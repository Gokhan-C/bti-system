Konu

The tariff classification of Utility Terrain Vehicles (UTVs) from Mexico

Eşyanın Tanımı (Türkçe özet)

10 Haziran 2026 tarihli yazınızda tarife sınıflandırma kararı talep etmiştiniz. Göz önünde bulundurulan öğeler, endüstriyel, ticari, kamu hizmetleri, kurtarma, belediye ve eğlence uygulamalarında arazi kullanımı için tasarlandığını belirttiğiniz dört (4) arazi aracı (UTV) modeli olarak tanımlanmıştır. Bu araçların hiçbiri caddelerde kullanıma uygun değildir ve hiçbiri kamuya açık otoyollarda kullanılmak üzere tasarlanmamıştır. İlk aracın Can-Am Defender XU olduğu belirlendi. Seçilebilir iki tekerlekten çekiş (2WD) ve dört tekerlekten çekiş (4WD) modlarına ve otomatik kilitlenen bir ön diferansiyele sahiptir. 95 hp ve 70 lb.-ft güce sahip dört tekerlekli bir ticari araçtır. 999 cc'lik içten yanmalı motorla güçlendirilmiş ve iş odaklı ortamlarda kullanılmak üzere tasarlanmıştır. Araç, BRP tarafından çiftçilik ve çiftçilik kategorisi altında pazarlanıyor ve öncelikle kısa mesafeli mal taşımacılığını içeren tarımsal uygulamalara yönelik. Araç sürücü dahil iki kişiliktir. 1.000 lbs'ye kadar kargo kutusu kapasitesi sunar. (454 kg), 1.500 lbs taşıma kapasitesi. (680 kg) ve 2.500 lbs'ye kadar çekme kapasitesi. (1.134 kg). İkinci aracın ise Can-Am Defender XU MAX olduğu belirlendi. Seçilebilir 2WD ve 4WD modlarının yanı sıra otomatik kilitlenen ön diferansiyele sahip gelişmiş çekiş kontrol sistemine sahiptir. 95 hp ve 70 lb.-ft güce sahip dört tekerlekli bir ticari araçtır. 999 cc'lik içten yanmalı motorla güçlendirilmiş ve iş odaklı ortamlarda kullanılmak üzere tasarlanmıştır. Zorlu çalışma ortamları, çiftlik yönetimi ve mülk bakımı için özel olarak tasarlanmış, ağır hizmet tipi, 6 yolculu bir UTV'dir. İki sıra VERSA-PRO bank oturma düzenine uyacak şekilde uzatılmış bir dingil mesafesi, 4.500 lb. vinç ve agresif çekiş lastiklerine sahiptir. Araç

Sınıflandırmanın Gerekçesi (Türkçe özet)

Can-Am Defender XU, Can-Am Defender XU MAX, Can-Am Defender XU PRO ve Can-Am Defender XU 6x6 için geçerli alt pozisyon 8704.31.0120, HTSUS olacaktır ve bu başlık şu şekilde olacaktır: "Malların taşınmasına yönelik motorlu taşıtlar: Diğer, yalnızca kıvılcım ateşlemeli içten yanmalı pistonlu motorlu: G.V.W. 5 metrik tonu aşmayan: G.V.W. değil 2,5 metrik tonu aşan” Genel vergi oranı değer üzerinden %25 olacaktır.

  Mr. Rachid Lakhal  BRP US INC  10101 Science Drive  Sturtevant, WI 53177  RE: The tariff classification of Utility Terrain Vehicles (UTVs) from Mexico  Dear Mr. Lakhal:  In your letter dated June 10, 2026, you requested a tariff classification ruling.  The items under consideration have been identified as four (4) models of utility terrain vehicles (UTVs),  which you state are designed for off-road use in industrial, commercial, utility, rescue, municipal, and  recreational applications. None of these vehicles are street-legal and are none are intended for operation on  public highways.  The first vehicle has been identified as the Can-Am Defender XU. It features a selectable two-wheel drive  (2WD) and four-wheel drive (4WD) modes, and an auto-locking front differential. It is a four-wheeled utility  vehicle with 95 hp and 70 lb.-ft. powered by a 999-cc internal combustion engine and designed for use in  work-oriented environments. The vehicle is marketed by BRP under its ranching and farming category and is  intended primarily for agricultural applications involving the short-distance transport of goods. The vehicle is  for two people, including the driver. It offers a cargo box capacity of up to 1,000 lbs. (454 kg), a payload  capacity of 1,500 lbs. (680 kg), and a towing capacity of up to 2,500 lbs. (1,134 kg).  The second vehicle has been identified as the Can-Am Defender XU MAX. It features a selectable 2WD and  4WD modes, as well as an advanced traction control system with an auto-locking front differential. It is a  four-wheeled utility vehicle with 95 hp and 70 lb.-ft. powered by a 999-cc internal combustion engine and  designed for use in work-oriented environments. It is a heavy-duty, 6-passenger UTV purpose-built for harsh  work environments, farm management, and property maintenance. It features an extended wheelbase to fit  two rows of VERSA-PRO bench seating, a 4,500 lb. winch, and aggressive traction tires. The vehicle is  equipped with a rear cargo box designed for the transport of tools, materials, and equipment. It offers a cargo  capacity of up to 1,000 lbs. (454 kg), a payload capacity of 1,445 lbs. (655 kg), and a towing capacity of up  to 2,500 lbs. (1,134 kg). 

The third vehicle has been identified as the Can-Am Defender XU PRO. It features a selectable 2WD and  4WD modes and an auto-locking front differential. It is a four-wheeled utility vehicle powered by a 976-cc  internal combustion engine and designed for use in work-related environments. It has a 3-person  VERSA-PRO bench seat. The vehicle is marketed by BRP under its Ranching and Farming category and is  intended primarily for agricultural applications involving the short-distance transport of goods. The vehicle is  equipped with an extended cargo box designed for the transport of tools, materials, and equipment within  confined or off-road work sites. It offers a cargo capacity of up to 1,000 lbs. (454 kg), a payload capacity of  1,570 lbs. (712 kg), and a towing capacity of up to 2,500 lbs. (1,134 kg). The cargo box can be hydraulically  tilted to facilitate the unloading of materials.  The fourth vehicle has been identified as the Can-Am Defender XU 6×6, a six-wheeled utility vehicle  powered by a 976-cc internal combustion engine and designed for use in work-related environments. The  vehicle is marketed by BRP under its ranching and farming category and is intended primarily for  agricultural applications involving the short-distance transport of goods. It has an extended cargo box  designed for the transport of tools, materials, and equipment within confined or off-road work sites. It offers  a cargo capacity of up to 1,000 lbs. (454 kg), a payload capacity of 1,570 lbs. (712 kg), and a towing capacity  of up to 3,000 lbs. (1,360 kg). The cargo box is capable of being hydraulically tilted to facilitate the  unloading of materials.  In your request you suggest classification of your UTVs in the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United  States (HTSUS) in either Heading 8704, as a vehicle for the transport of goods, or in 8709, as a work truck,  not fitted with lifting and handling equipment.  In regard to your proposed classification in 8709, we  disagree.  The Explanatory Notes (ENs) to the Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System, which  represent the official interpretation of the tariff at the international level, facilitate classification under the  HTSUS by offering guidance in understanding the scope of the headings and the GRIs.  Vehicles of 8709 are described as “vehicles of the types used in factories, warehouses, dock areas or airports  for the short distance transport of various loads (goods or containers) or, on railway station platforms, to haul  small trailers.” You state in your submission that the UTVs at issue are suitable for off-the-road uses and  principally used on farms to transport personnel, haul fertilizer, sand, etc., and for property maintenance.  Neither of which are akin to the locations enumerated in the ENs to 87.09. In addition, the Defender XU Pro  and XU 6x6 are equipped with hydraulic lift beds, which has historically been considered “lifting and  handling” equipment.  The applicable subheading for the Can-Am Defender XU, Can-Am Defender XU MAX, Can-Am Defender  XU PRO, and Can-Am Defender XU 6x6 will be 8704.31.0120, HTSUS, which provides for “Motor vehicles  for the transport of goods: Other, with only spark-ignition internal combustion piston engine: G.V.W. not  exceeding 5 metric tons: G.V.W. not exceeding 2.5 metric tons” The general rate of duty will be 25% ad  valorem.  The duties cited above are current as of this ruling’s issuance. Duty rates are provided for your convenience  and are subject to change. The text of the most recent HTSUS and the accompanying duty rates are provided  at https://hts.usitc.gov/.  This ruling does not address the applicability of any additional duties, taxes, fees, exactions and/or other  charges, which may apply to the goods discussed herein. This includes, but is not limited to, tariffs and other  duties as provided for in Subchapter III to Chapter 99, HTSUS. Thus, for example, in addition to the  classification stated above, the merchandise covered by this ruling may also need to be reported with either  the Chapter 99 provision under which an additional tariff applies or one of the Chapter 99 provisions  covering exceptions to such tariffs.
