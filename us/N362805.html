Konu

The tariff classification of handbags from Mexico

Eşyanın Tanımı (Türkçe özet)

1 Temmuz 2026 tarihli yazınızda tarife sınıflandırma kararı talep etmiştiniz. İncelememiz için fotoğraflar ve açıklayıcı literatür gönderdiniz. Söz konusu makaleler beş farklı çanta stilinden oluşuyor. Her çantanın dış yüzeyi sığır derisinden yapılmıştır. Normalde günlük olarak taşınan küçük kişisel eşyalara organizasyon, koruma, taşınabilirlik ve depolama sağlayacak şekilde tasarlanmış ve boyutlandırılmıştır. Style Sette Negra Granulada, öpücük kilitli ve pilili silueti olan çerçeveli bir el çantasıdır. Ölçüleri 17,7 inç (U) × 10,6 inç (Y) × 9,1 inç (D). Style Sette Lungo, öpücük kilitli, uzun çerçeveli bir el çantasıdır. Ölçüleri 15,4 inç (U) × 6,7 inç (Y) ve 4,7 inç (G). Style Sette Petito, öpücük kilitli ve zincir omuz askılı çerçeveli bir el çantasıdır. Ölçüleri 8,3 inç (U) × 6,3 inç (Y) × 4,7 inç (D). Style Aluna, öpücük kilidi ve kavisli silueti olan çerçeveli bir el çantasıdır. (L) 13,4 inç × (H) 7,1 inç boyutlarındadır. Style Riga, deri saçaklı, kapak tarzı bir el çantasıdır. Ölçüleri 5,4 inç (U) × 7,5 inç (Y) × 2,0 inç (G).

Sınıflandırmanın Gerekçesi (Türkçe özet)

Değeri 20 ABD dolarını aşmayan sığır derisi el çantaları için geçerli alt pozisyon, el çantalarını öngören Amerika Birleşik Devletleri Uyumlaştırılmış Tarife Tablosu (HTSUS) 4202.21.6000 olacaktır.

sapsız olanlar da dahil olmak üzere omuz askılı, dış yüzeyi deriden veya terkip yoluyla elde edilen deriden olan, diğerinin değeri 20 doları geçmeyen. Genel vergi oranı ad valorem olarak %10 olacaktır. Değeri 20 Doların üzerinde olan sığır derisinden el çantaları için geçerli alt pozisyon, omuz askılı olsun ya da olmasın, sapsız olanlar da dahil olmak üzere, dış yüzeyi deriden ya da bileşim deriden olan, her biri 20 Doların üzerinde değere sahip el çantalarını öngören 4202.21.9000, HTSUS olacaktır. Genel vergi oranı ad valorem olarak %9 olacaktır.

Av Mexico 2798, 1A, Circunvalación Vallarta  Guadalajara 44680  Mexico  RE:  The tariff classification of handbags from Mexico  Dear Ms. Alcocer:  In your letter dated July 1, 2026, you requested a tariff classification ruling. You have submitted photographs  and descriptive literature for our review.  The articles at issue are five styles of handbags.  Each bag is constructed with an outer surface of bovine  leather. They are designed and sized to provide organization, protection, portability, and storage to the small  personal effects normally carried on a daily basis.  Style Sette Negra Granulada is a framed clutch handbag with kiss-lock closure and pleated silhouette.  It measures 17.7 in (L) × 10.6 in (H) × 9.1 in (D).  Style Sette Lungo is an elongated framed clutch handbag with kiss-lock closure.  It measures 15.4 in (L) × 6.7 in (H) 4.7 in (W).  Style Sette Petito is a framed handbag with kiss-lock closure and a chain shoulder strap.  It measures 8.3 in (L) × 6.3 in (H) × 4.7 in (D).  Style Aluna is a framed clutch with a kiss-lock closure and curved silhouette.  It measures 13.4 in (L) × 7.1 in (H).  Style Riga is flap-style clutch handbag with leather fringe.  It measures 5.4 in (L) × 7.5 in (H) × 2.0 in (W).  The applicable subheading for the bovine leather handbags, if valued not over $20, will be 4202.21.6000,  Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS), which provides for handbags, whether or not 

with shoulder strap, including those without handle, with outer surface of leather or of composition leather,  other, valued not over $20 each. The general rate of duty will be 10% ad valorem.  The applicable subheading for the bovine leather handbags, if valued over $20, will be 4202.21.9000,  HTSUS, which provides for handbags, whether or not with shoulder strap, including those without handle,  with outer surface of leather or of composition leather, other, valued over $20 each. The general rate of duty  will be 9% ad valorem.  The duties cited above are current as of this ruling’s issuance.  Duty rates are provided for your convenience  and are subject to change. The text of the most recent HTSUS and the accompanying duty rates are provided  at https://hts.usitc.gov/.  This ruling does not address the applicability of any additional duties, taxes, fees, exactions and/or other  charges, which may apply to the goods discussed herein.  This includes, but is not limited to, tariffs and other  duties as provided for in Subchapter III to Chapter 99, HTSUS.  Thus, for example, in addition to the  classification stated above, the merchandise covered by this ruling may also need to be reported with either  the Chapter 99 provision under which an additional tariff applies or one of the Chapter 99 provisions  covering exceptions to such tariffs.  For further information to assist with the importation process, please refer to the frequently updated Cargo  Systems Messaging Service (CSMS) messages at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/automated/cargo-systems-messaging-service and the Trade Remedies page at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/programs-administration/trade-remedies.  The holding set forth above applies only to the specific factual situation and merchandise description as  identified in the ruling request.  This position is clearly set forth in Title 19, Code of Federal Regulations  (CFR), Section 177.9(b)(1).  This section states that a ruling letter is issued on the assumption that all of the  information furnished in the ruling letter, whether directly, by reference, or by implication, is accurate and  complete in every material respect.  In the event that the facts are modified in any way, or if the goods do not  conform to these facts at time of importation, you should bring this to the attention of U.S. Customs and  Border Protection (CBP) and submit a request for a new ruling in accordance with 19 CFR 177.2.    Additionally, we note that the material facts described in the foregoing ruling may be subject to periodic  verification by CBP.  This ruling is being issued under the provisions of Part 177 of the Customs and Border Protection  Regulations (19 C.F.R. 177).  A copy of the ruling or the control number indicated above should be provided with the entry documents  filed at the time this merchandise is imported.
