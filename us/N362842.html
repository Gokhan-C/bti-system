Konu

The tariff classification of an automated lumber bundle formation system from Canada

Eşyanın Tanımı (Türkçe özet)

2 Temmuz 2026 tarihli mektubunuzda, müşteriniz Machinage Piche Incorporated adına tarife sınıflandırma kararı talep etmişsiniz. Söz konusu Otomatik Kereste Demetini Oluşturma Sistemi, kereste demetleri oluşturmak üzere tasarlanmıştır. Kereste demetleri, yukarı ve aşağı yöndeki konveyörler ve diğer makineler arasına konumlandırılan otomatik bir istifleme makinesi tarafından oluşturulur. Toplam boyutundan dolayı sistem, tüm modüller tek sevkiyatta ithal edilecek şekilde montaja hazır modüller halinde ithal edilecektir. İlk bölüm, iki Çekme Zinciri Modülü, Otomatik Kereste Dağıtım Modülü ve Kereste Akışı Biriktirme Modülü olarak adlandırılan bir dizi konveyörden oluşur. Taşıma Zinciri Modülleri, keresteyi yukarı akışlı bir Ayırıcı Modülden (sistemle birlikte içe aktarılmayan), raylar üzerinde yatay olarak hareket eden bir platform üzerine monte edilmiş zincir tipi bir konveyör olan Otomatik Kereste Dağıtım Modülüne taşır. Bu platform keresteyi, keresteyi Çözücüye ileten sabit zincir tipi bir konveyör olan Kereste Akışı Biriktirme Modülüne aktarır. Unscrambler, rastgele biriken kereste parçalarını paralel ve eşit aralıklı bir yönde düzenleyerek bunları daha sonraki otomatik demet oluşturma işlemlerine hazırlar. Bu makine "tekli" veya "çiftli" olarak yapılandırılabilir; çiftli konfigürasyon dikey olarak istiflenmiş iki tekli üniteden oluşur, ancak bunun dışında işlev açısından aynıdır. Bir sonraki makine grubu, her ikisi de özel konveyörler olan Hızlandırma Zincirlerini ve Paket Oluşturucu Biriktirme Aktarma Ünitelerini içerir. Hızlandırma Zincirleri, Unscrambler'dan çıkarken kereste kalasların aralığını, zamanlamasını ve sıralamasını düzenler. Paket Oluşturucu Biriktirme Aktarma Birimi, zincir tipi

konveyör, kalasları belirtilen paket kuruşuna göre düzenler

Sınıflandırmanın Gerekçesi (Türkçe özet)

Otomatik Kereste Demeti Oluşturma Sistemi için geçerli alt pozisyon, "Diğer kaldırma, taşıma, yükleme veya boşaltma makineleri (örneğin asansörler, yürüyen merdivenler, konveyörler, teleferikler): Diğer makineler: Diğer" ifadesini öngören 8428.90.0390, HTSUS olacaktır. Genel vergi oranı ücretsiz olacaktır. Mal, ithal durumuna göre sınıflandırılacaktır. 19 CFR § 141.51 ve 19 CFR § 141.58 gerekliliklerinin karşılanmaması durumunda, sistemin her bir bileşeni kendi ilgili pozisyonunda ayrı ayrı sınıflandırılacaktır. Görev oranları size kolaylık sağlamak amacıyla verilmiştir ve değişebilir. En güncel HTSUS metni ve beraberindeki vergi oranları https://hts.usitc.gov/ adresinde sunulmaktadır.

Deringer  173 West Service Road  Champlain, NY 12919  RE:  The tariff classification of an automated lumber bundle formation system from Canada  Dear Ms. McLaughlin:  In your letter dated July 2, 2026, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client,  Machinage Piche Incorporated.  The subject Automated Lumber Bundle Formation System is engineered to create lumber bundles. The  lumber bundles are formed by an automated stacking machine that is positioned between upstream and  downstream conveyors and other machines. Due to its overall size, the system will be imported as modules  ready for assembly, with all modules imported in a single shipment.  The first section consists of a series of conveyors referred to as two Haul Out Chain Modules, an Automated  Lumber Distribution Module, and a Lumber Flow Accumulation Module. The Haul Out Chain Modules  transport lumber from an upstream Sorter Module (not imported with the system) to the Automated Lumber  Distribution Module, which is a chain-type conveyor mounted on a platform that moves horizontally on rails.  This platform transfers lumber to the Lumber Flow Accumulation Module, a stationary chain-type conveyor  that conveys the lumber to the Unscrambler.  The Unscrambler arranges randomly accumulated lumber pieces into a parallel and evenly-spaced  orientation, preparing them for downstream automated bundle-forming operations. This machine may be  configured as a “single” or “double,” with the double configuration consisting of two single units stacked  vertically, but otherwise identical in function.  The next group of machines includes Speed Up Chains and Package Maker Accumulation Transfer Units,  both specialized conveyors. The Speed Up Chains regulate the spacing, timing, and sequencing of lumber  planks as they exit the Unscrambler. The Package Maker Accumulation Transfer Unit, a chain-type 

conveyor, arranges the planks according to specified bundle dimensions. This is accomplished by adjusting  conveyor speed and buffering planks into organized rows, which are then transferred to the Package Maker  for bundle assembly.  The Package Maker is the core of the automated bundle-forming system, responsible for aligning, stacking,  and bundling the incoming rows of planks. Incorporated into this unit are two submodules: the Pressing Foot  module, which secures planks in place during bundling, and the Secondary Lift module, located in the lower  section of the Package Maker. The Secondary Lift module lowers the assembled bundles, enabling the  Package Maker to continue stacking rows and maintain uninterrupted automated stacking cycles.  The final group of machines includes an Outfeed Bundle Synchronization Module, Package Outfeed Rollers,  a Strapping Machine, a Strapping Machine Infeed Roller, a Strapping Machine Outfeed Transfer, a Package  Doubler, and an Outfeed Package Transfer. The Outfeed Bundle Synchronization Module, Package Outfeed  Rollers, Strapping Machine Infeed Roller, Strapping Machine Outfeed Transfer, and Outfeed Package  Transfer are all conveyors. The Strapping Machine straps the bundles before they are conveyed to the  Package Doubler, which stacks the bundles for retrieval by a forklift operator.  The system also incorporates a Makeup Deck and Re-entry Bundle Sequencing Module to handle rejected or  reprocessed lumber. Both units are chain-type conveyors designed to transport and reintegrate rejected  lumber back into the system.  In accordance with Note 4 to Section XVI, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS), we  consider the subject Automated Lumber Bundle System with all of its interconnected modules to be a  functional unit. The clearly defined function is the stacking of lumber planks into a bundle.  The applicable subheading for the Automated Lumber Bundle Formation System will be 8428.90.0390,  HTSUS, which provides for “Other lifting, handling, loading or unloading machinery (for example, elevators,  escalators, conveyors, teleferics): Other machinery: Other.” The general rate of duty will be free.  Merchandise is to be classified according to its condition as imported. Should the requirements of 19 CFR §  141.51 and 19 CFR § 141.58 not be met, each component of the system would be separately classified in its  own respective heading.  Duty rates are provided for your convenience and are subject to change. The text of the most recent HTSUS  and the accompanying duty rates are provided at https://hts.usitc.gov/.  This ruling does not address the applicability of any additional duties, taxes, fees, exactions and/or other  charges, which may apply to the goods discussed herein.  This includes, but is not limited to, tariffs and other  duties as provided for in Subchapter III to Chapter 99, HTSUS.  Thus, for example, in addition to the  classification stated above, the merchandise covered by this ruling may also need to be reported with either  the Chapter 99 provision under which an additional tariff applies or one of the Chapter 99 provisions  covering exceptions to such tariffs.  For further information to assist with the importation process, please refer to the frequently updated Cargo  Systems Messaging Service (CSMS) messages at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/automated/cargo-systems-messaging-service and the Trade Remedies page at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/programs-administration/trade-remedies.
