The tariff classification of metal furniture from Germany

Eşyanın Tanımı (Türkçe özet)

In your letter dated
July 28, 2026
, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of Rittal NorthAmerica LLC. In lieu of samples, technical and illustrative literature, a product description, and amanufacturing synopsis were provided for review.Item TS8806.500, the “TS 8 Baying System,” is a series of floor-standing, empty, and unequipped steelstorage cabinets that measure approximately 31.5" in width, 78.7" in height, and 23.6" in depth. Theassembled cabinet will contain powder coated vertical and horizontal panels, vertical upright mounting rails,a single hinged front door, roof plate, base plate, and a mounting plate. The “TS 8 Baying System” willpermit multiple cabinets to be joined (bayed) on the left or right sides to create larger modularconfigurations. The cabinet is designed to secure computer server equipment, circuit breakers, busbars,fuses, relays, networking devices, power-distribution units, power supplies, switching devices, wiring andadditional hardware components (sold separately). The ruling request seeks classification of the subject merchandise in subheading 9403.10.0040, HarmonizedTariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS). We agree.

Sınıflandırmanın Gerekçesi (Türkçe özet)

The applicable subheading for the subject merchandise will be 9403.10.0040, HTSUS, which provides for“Other furniture and parts thereof: Metal furniture of a kind used in offices: Other.” The general rate ofduty will be free.
