Konu

The tariff classification of prefabricated modules from Thailand

Eşyanın Tanımı (Türkçe özet)

22 Mayıs 2026 tarihli yazınızda tarife sınıflandırma kararı talep etmiştiniz. Söz konusu prefabrik modüller Teksas'taki bir petrokimya üretim tesisine kalıcı olarak kurulacak. Modüller, etan ham maddesini etilen ve hidrokarbon ürünlerine dönüştürmek için yüksek sıcaklıkta kırma kullanan bir termal işlem ünitesi olarak tanımlanan bir etan kırma fırını oluşturmak üzere kazık temelleri üzerine dikey olarak istiflenecek şekilde tasarlanmıştır. Fırının içinde etan, yüksek sıcaklıklarda buharla karıştırılır; bu, moleküler bağları kıran ve daha hafif hidrokarbon ürünleri oluşturan bir işlemdir. Tek bir sevkiyatta toplam on modül birlikte ithal edilecektir. Fırın XXIF-01 olarak adlandırılacak olan bitmiş fırın, etan işleme kompleksindeki mevcut sekiz fırından oluşan bir gruba bağlanacak. İlk modüle Alt Radyant Bölüm veya Ateş Kutusu adı verilir. Bu modül, genel etan kırma fırınının alt bölümünü oluşturur ve tabanının altında brülörlerle donatılmış, refrakter kaplı bir odadan oluşacaktır. Bu modülün işlevi, reaksiyonu başlatmak için dikey radyant tüpler aracılığıyla gönderilen yoğun radyant ısı üretmektir. Radyant tüpler ithalattan sonra takılır ve bu talebin bir parçası değildir. İkinci modül Üst Radyant Bölüm olarak anılır ve doğrudan birinci modülün üzerine monte edilir. Bu modül, Alt Radyant Bölümün bir uzantısı olarak işlev görür ve yapısal bir çerçeveye monte edilmiş, refrakter kaplı bir odadan oluşur. Radyant tüpleri de ithalattan sonra takılacaktır ve bu talebin bir parçası değildir. Üçüncü ve dördüncü modüller Konveksiyon Bölümleri olarak anılır. Üst üste istiflenirler ve ethayı önceden ısıtmak üzere yapılandırılmışlardır.

  Ruby Medrano  ExxonMobil Product Solutions Company  22777 Springwoods Village Parkway  Spring, TX 77389  RE:  The tariff classification of prefabricated modules from Thailand  Dear Ms. Medrano:  In your letter dated May 22, 2026, you requested a tariff classification ruling.  The subject prefabricated modules will be permanently installed at a petrochemical manufacturing facility in  Texas. The modules are designed to be stacked vertically on piling foundations to form an ethane cracking  furnace, which is described as a thermal processing unit that uses high-temperature cracking to convert  ethane feedstock into ethylene and hydrocarbon products. Within the furnace, ethane is mixed with steam at  elevated temperatures, a process that breaks molecular bonds and creates lighter hydrocarbon products. A  total of ten modules will be imported together in a single shipment. The finished furnace, which will be  called Furnace XXIF-01, will be connected to a group of eight existing furnaces at the ethane processing  complex.  The first module is referred to as the Lower Radiant Section or Firebox. This module forms the lower section  of the overall ethane cracking furnace and will consist of a refractory-lined chamber fitted with burners  underneath its floor. The function of this module is to generate intense radiant heat, which is sent through  vertical radiant tubes to drive the reaction. The radiant tubes are installed after importation and are not part of  this request.  The second module is referred to as the Upper Radiant Section and is installed directly above the first  module. This module functions as an extension of the Lower Radiant Section and consists of a  refractory-lined chamber mounted in a structural frame. Its radiant tubes will also be installed after  importation and are not part of this request.  The third and fourth modules are referred to as Convection Sections. They are stacked one on top of the other  and configured to both pre-heat the ethane feed and to superheat very high-pressure steam. Flue gas is used as  a heat source to raise and lower the temperature of the feed and steam, transferring thermal energy between 

the flue gas, the feed, and the steam. At the time of importation, these two modules will each contain a  refractory-lined chamber and structural supports for the heat-exchange tubes. The internal components, such  as the primary heat-exchange tubes, secondary heat exchanger, and custom valves, are sourced separately and  installed onsite.  Also included is a separate module referred to as the Control Valve Deck, which is described as a steel  structural platform that will be attached to the third module in the United States. This platform allows  physical access to module three and does not contain any mechanical equipment.  The fifth module consists of a high-pressure steam drum and an induced draft fan mounted in a structural  frame. This module is mounted directly on top of the fourth module. The steam drum is described as a  carbon-steel pressure vessel designed to recover heat from the furnace flue gases. The function of the fan is to  maintain controlled negative pressure within the furnace by continuously drawing flue gases through the  radiant and convection sections.  The sixth module is referred to as the Stack and is described as a vertical exhaust chimney that will be  mounted atop the convection section of the fifth module. Its function is to vent flue gases, manage emissions,  and create a draft (natural or induced) to draw combustion gases from the burners up through the furnace.  The last module is referred to as a Stair Tower Module that is assembled to the side of the furnace. This  module consists of multiple flights of stairs in an open frame. The stair tower module exists solely to allow  physical access to the furnace levels and does not contain any processing equipment.  You also requested a classification ruling on two Interconnecting Piping Modules which will be used to  connect the subject furnace to the existing furnaces in the complex. These modules will be addressed in a  separate ruling.  In your submission, you suggest that the Lower Radiant Section, Upper Radiant Section, Convection  Sections, Stack, and Very High Pressure Steam Drum and Induced Draft Fan Module are classifiable in  heading 8419, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS). You also suggest that the Control  Valve Deck and the Stair Tower Module are classifiable in heading 7308, HTSUS. We agree.  The applicable subheading for the Lower Radiant Section, Upper Radiant Section, Convection Sections,  Stack, and Very High Pressure Steam Drum and Induced Draft Fan Module will be 8419.89.9585, HTSUS,  which provides for “Machinery, plant or laboratory equipment, whether or not electrically heated (excluding  furnaces, ovens and other equipment of heading 8514), for the treatment of materials by a process involving a  change of temperature such as heating, cooking, roasting, distilling, rectifying, sterilizing, pasteurizing,  steaming, drying, evaporating, vaporizing, condensing or cooling, other than machinery or plant of a kind  used for domestic purposes; instantaneous or storage water heaters, nonelectric; parts thereof: Other  machinery, plant or equipment: Other: Other: Other: Other: For other materials.” The general rate of duty  will be 4.2 percent ad valorem.  The applicable subheading for the Control Valve Deck and the Stair Tower Module will be 7308.90.9590,  HTSUS, which provides for “Structures (excluding prefabricated buildings of heading 9406) and parts of  structures (for example, bridges and bridge sections, lock gates, towers, lattice masts, roofs, roofing  frameworks, doors and windows and their frames and thresholds for doors, shutters, balustrades, pillars and  columns) of iron or steel; plates, rods, angles, shapes, sections, tubes and the like, prepared for use in  structures, of iron or steel: Other: Other: Other: Other: Other.” The general rate of duty will be free.  Duty rates are provided for your convenience and are subject to change. The text of the most recent HTSUS  and the accompanying duty rates are provided at https://hts.usitc.gov/. 

Merchandise is to be classified according to its condition as imported. Should the requirements of 19 CFR §  141.51 and 19 CFR § 141.58 not be met, each component of the system would be separately classified in its  own respective heading.  This ruling does not address the applicability of any additional duties, taxes, fees, exactions and/or other  charges, which may apply to the goods discussed herein.  This includes, but is not limited to, tariffs and other  duties as provided for in Subchapter III to Chapter 99, HTSUS.  Thus, for example, in addition to the  classification stated above, the merchandise covered by this ruling may also need to be reported with either  the Chapter 99 provision under which an additional tariff applies or one of the Chapter 99 provisions  covering exceptions to such tariffs.
