Konu

The tariff classification of stone pavers and copings from Turkey.

Eşyanın Tanımı (Türkçe özet)

31 Mart 2026 tarihli yazınızda tarife sınıflandırma kararı talep etmiştiniz. Söz konusu ürünler, Kabuk Bej Kireçtaşı Eskitme Taşı, Diana Royal Mermer Deri Kaplamalı Döşeme, Diana Royal Mermer Kumlanmış Kaplama, Diana Royal Mermer Deri Kaplamalı Bullnose Kaplama ve Tundra Gri Mermer Derili Kolaylaştırılmış Kenar Kaplama olarak adlandırılıyor. Numuneler, karar talebinizle birlikte iletilmiş ve analiz için Gümrük ve Sınır Koruma Laboratuvarı'na iletilmiştir. Bu analiz tamamlandı. Sağladığınız bilgilere göre, bu taşların tümü, 6 inç uzunluk, 6 inç genişlik, 32 inç uzunluk ve 32 inç genişlik arasında değişen birden fazla boyutta mevcut. Üç tarz Döşeme Taşının kalınlığı yaklaşık 3 santimetredir ve dış mekan yürüyüş yolları ve verandalarda kullanılmak üzere tasarlanmıştır. Diana Royal Mermer Derili Boğa Burunlu Başlık yaklaşık 3 santimetre kalınlığındayken Tundra Gri Mermer Derili Kolaylaştırılmış Kenar Başlığı yaklaşık 5 santimetre kalınlığındadır; her ikisi de yer altı havuz başa çıkma için tasarlanmıştır. Beş taş stilinin tamamı kullanıma ve kuruluma hazır olarak ithal edilmektedir. Kabuk Bej Kireçtaşı Eskitme Taşı krem-bej kıvrımları ve benekleriyle fildişi renginde bir taştır. Laboratuvar analizleri cila alabilen kireç taşından oluştuğunu tespit etti. Üst kısmı tamburlu bir yüzeye sahiptir ve yanlar basitçe kesilir veya kesilir. Diana Royal Mermer Deri Döşeme Taşı, gri benekli ve benekli, kırık beyaz bir taştır. Laboratuvar analizleri cila alabilen kireç taşından oluştuğunu tespit etti. Üst kısım deri kaplıdır ve yanlar basitçe kesilmiş veya kesilmiştir.

Diana Royal Mermer Kumlanmış Finişer, gri benekli, fildişi renginde bir taştır. Laboratuvar analizi

Sınıflandırmanın Gerekçesi (Türkçe özet)

Kabuk Bej Kireç Taşı Eskitme Taşı, Diana Royal Mermer Deri Kaplama Taşı ve Diana Royal Mermer Kumlanmış Kaplama Taşı için geçerli alt pozisyon, “Doğal taştan setler, bordür taşları ve kaldırım taşları (arduvaz hariç)” öngören Amerika Birleşik Devletleri Harmonize Tarife Tablosu (HTSUS) 6801.00.0000 olacaktır. Genel vergi oranı ad valorem olarak yüzde 2,8 olacaktır. Diana Royal Mermer Derili Bullnose Başlık için geçerli alt pozisyon 6802.91.1500, HTSUS olacaktır ve bu pozisyon “İşlenmiş anıtsal veya inşaat taşları (arduvaz hariç) ve bunlardan eşya (6801 pozisyonundaki ürünler hariç…: Diğer: Mermer, traverten ve kaymaktaşı: Mermer: Diğer”). Genel vergi oranı değer üzerinden yüzde 4,9 olacak. Tundra Gri Mermer Derili Kolaylaştırılmış Kenar Başlık için geçerli alt pozisyon 6802.92.0000, HTSUS olacaktır ve bu alt pozisyon, "6801 pozisyonundaki ürünler hariç işlenmiş anıtsal veya inşaat taşları (arduvaz hariç) ve bunlardan eşya: Diğer kalkerli taşlar" ifadesini sağlar. Genel vergi oranı değer üzerinden yüzde 4,9 olacak.

  Umit Ciltas  Corumar LLC  3361 Rouse Road  Orlando, FL 32817  RE:  The tariff classification of stone pavers and copings from Turkey.  Dear Mr. Ciltas:  In your letter dated March 31, 2026, you requested a tariff classification ruling.  The merchandise under consideration is referred to as Shell Beige Limestone Tumbled Paver, Diana Royal  Marble Leathered Paver, Diana Royal Marble Sandblasted Paver, Diana Royal Marble Leathered Bullnose  Coping, and Tundra Gray Marble Leathered Eased Edge Coping.  Samples were submitted with your ruling  request and were forwarded to the Customs and Border Protection Laboratory for analysis.  This analysis has  been completed.   From the information you provided, these stones are all available in multiple dimensions ranging from 6  inches long by 6 inches wide to 32 inches long by 32 inches wide.  The three styles of Pavers measure  approximately 3 centimeters in thickness, and are designed for use in outdoor walkways and patios.  The  Diana Royal Marble Leathered Bullnose Coping measures approximately 3 centimeters thick, while the  Tundra Gray Marble Leathered Eased Edge Coping measures approximately 5 centimeters thick; they are  both designed for in-ground pool coping.  All five styles of stone are imported ready to use and install.   The Shell Beige Limestone Tumbled Paver is an ivory-colored stone with cream to beige swirls and  mottling.  Laboratory analysis has determined that it is comprised of limestone capable of taking a polish.   The top has a tumbled finish and the sides are simply cut or sawn.    The Diana Royal Marble Leathered Paver is an off-white stone with gray speckles and mottling.  Laboratory  analysis has determined that it is comprised of limestone capable of taking a polish.  The top has a leathered  finish and the sides are simply cut or sawn.  

The Diana Royal Marble Sandblasted Paver is an ivory-colored stone with gray speckles.  Laboratory  analysis has determined that it is comprised of limestone capable of taking a polish.  The top has a  sandblasted finish and the sides are simply cut or sawn.   The Diana Royal Marble Leathered Bullnose Coping is an ivory-colored stone with gray speckles.   Laboratory analysis has determined that it is comprised of limestone capable of taking a polish.  The top has  a leathered finish.  One edge of the stone has a polished bullnose coping, while the other three sides are  simply cut or sawn.   The Tundra Gray Marble Leathered Eased Edge Coping is a light to dark gray stone with white streaks and  mottling.  Laboratory analysis has determined that it is comprised of dolomite.  The top of the stone has a  leathered finish.  One edge of the stone is polished with an eased edge coping, while the other three sides are  simply cut or sawn.    Intercontinental Marble Corp. v. United States, No. 03-1555 (Fed. Cir. 2004) held that the tariff provision for  marble also includes polished limestone or limestone capable of taking a polish.  As the Shell Beige  Limestone Tumbled Paver, Diana Royal Marble Leathered Paver, Diana Royal Marble Sandblasted Paver,  and Diana Royal Marble Leathered Bullnose Coping samples are limestone which have been polished or are  capable of taking a polish, they are considered marble for classification purposes.  The applicable subheading for the Shell Beige Limestone Tumbled Paver, Diana Royal Marble Leathered  Paver, and Diana Royal Marble Sandblasted Paver will be 6801.00.0000, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the  United States (HTSUS), which provides for “Setts, curbstones and flagstones, of natural stone (except slate).”   The general rate of duty will be 2.8 percent ad valorem.   The applicable subheading for the Diana Royal Marble Leathered Bullnose Coping will be 6802.91.1500,  HTSUS, which provides for “Worked monumental or building stone (except slate) and articles thereof, other  than goods of heading 6801…:  Other:  Marble, travertine and alabaster:  Marble:  Other.”  The general rate  of duty will be 4.9 percent ad valorem.  The applicable subheading for the Tundra Gray Marble Leathered Eased Edge Coping will be 6802.92.0000,  HTSUS, which provides for “Worked monumental or building stone (except slate) and articles thereof, other  than goods of heading 6801…:  Other calcareous stone.”  The general rate of duty will be 4.9 percent ad  valorem.    The duties cited above are current as of this ruling’s issuance.  Duty rates are provided for your convenience  and are subject to change.  The text of the most recent HTSUS and the accompanying duty rates are provided  at https://hts.usitc.gov/.  This ruling does not address the applicability of any additional duties, taxes, fees, exactions and/or other  charges, which may apply to the goods discussed herein.  This includes, but is not limited to, tariffs and other  duties as provided for in Subchapter III to Chapter 99, HTSUS.  Thus, for example, in addition to the  classification stated above, the merchandise covered by this ruling may also need to be reported with either  the Chapter 99 provision under which an additional tariff applies or one of the Chapter 99 provisions  covering exceptions to such tariffs.  For further information to assist with the importation process, please refer to the frequently updated Cargo  Systems Messaging Service (CSMS) messages at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/automated/cargo-systems-messaging-service and the Trade Remedies page at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/programs-administration/trade-remedies.  The holding set forth above applies only to the specific factual situation and merchandise description as  identified in the ruling request. (CFR), Section 177.9(b)(1).  This section states that a ruling letter is issued on the assumption that all of the  information furnished in the ruling letter, whether directly, by reference, or by implication, is accurate and  complete in every material respect.  In the event that the facts are modified in any way, or if the goods do not  conform to these facts at time of importation, you should bring this to the attention of U.S. Customs and  Border Protection (CBP) and submit a request for a new ruling in accordance with 19 CFR 177.2.  Additionally, we note that the material facts described in the foregoing ruling may be subject to periodic  verification by CBP.
