Konu

The tariff classification of multilayer wood flooring from Indonesia

Eşyanın Tanımı (Türkçe özet)

17 Temmuz 2026 tarihli mektubunuzda çok katlı ahşap döşemelere ilişkin bağlayıcı tarife sınıflandırması yapılmasını talep etmiştiniz. Ürün bilgileri ve görselleri incelememiz için gönderildi. Söz konusu ürünler, üç farklı mühendislik ürünü veya çok katmanlı ahşap döşeme stilidir. Stil 1, tamamı tropikal ahşaplardan oluşan çok sayıda Maun, Gmelina veya Akasya katmanından yapılmıştır. Resimde yedi ahşap katmanı gösteriliyor ve hiçbir katmanın kalınlığının 2,5 mm'yi aşmadığını belirtiyorsunuz. Her katmanın dokusu bitişik katmanlara 90 derecelik bir açıyla uzanır. Panelin üzeri melamin emdirilmiş baskılı kağıt tabakasıyla kaplanmıştır ve panelin arkası ışınlanmış çapraz bağlı polietilen (IXPE) köpükle kaplanmıştır. Hem kağıt hem de köpük, alttaki ahşabın damarlarını gizler. Panellerin kenarları ve uçları zıvanalı ve yivlidir. Stil 2, tümü tropik ahşaplardan oluşan çok sayıda Maun, Gmelina veya Akasya katmanından yapılmıştır. Resimde sekiz ahşap katmanı gösteriliyor ve hiçbir katmanın kalınlığının 2,5 mm'yi aşmadığını belirtiyorsunuz. Her katmanın dokusu bitişik katmanlara 90 derecelik bir açıyla uzanır. Panelin üzeri melamin emdirilmiş baskılı kağıt tabakasıyla kaplanmıştır. Kağıt, alttaki ahşabın damarlarını gizliyor. Panellerin kenarları ve uçları zıvanalı ve yivlidir. Stil 3, tamamı tropikal ahşaplardan oluşan Maun, Gmelina veya Akasya'nın birden fazla katmanından yapılmıştır. Resimde sekiz ahşap katmanı gösteriliyor ve hiçbir katmanın kalınlığının 2,5 mm'yi aşmadığını belirtiyorsunuz. Her katmanın dokusu bitişik katmanlara 90 derecelik bir açıyla uzanır. Panel, yüksek yoğunluklu bir sunta (HDF) tabakası ile kaplanmıştır ve bir melamin emdirilmiş baskılı kağıt tabakası ile kaplanmıştır. Kağıt ve HDF, alttaki ahşabın damarlarını gizler. Paneller dillidir

Sınıflandırmanın Gerekçesi (Türkçe özet)

Dış katı Maun ağacından olduğunda Stil 1, 2 ve 3 için geçerli alt pozisyon, Kontrplak, kaplamalı paneller ve benzeri lamine ahşapları öngören 4412.31.6100, HTSUS olacaktır: Yalnızca ahşap levhalardan (bambu hariç) oluşan, her katın kalınlığı 6 mm'yi geçmeyen diğer kontrplak: En az bir dış katı tropikal ahşaptan olan: Diğer: En az bir dış katı ahşaptan oluşan aşağıdaki tropikal ağaçlar: Koyu Kırmızı Meranti, Açık Kırmızı Meranti, Beyaz Lauan, Sipo, Limba, Okoumé, Obeche, Acajou d'Afrique, Sapelli, Virola, Maun, Palissandre de Para, Palissandre de Rio veya Palissandre de Rose. Genel vergi oranı ad valorem olarak yüzde 8 olacaktır.

Bir ön katı Gmelina veya Acacia olduğunda Stil 1, 2 ve 3 için geçerli alt pozisyon, Kontrplak, kaplamalı paneller ve benzeri lamine ahşapları öngören 4412.31.9200, HTSUS olacaktır: Yalnızca ahşap levhalardan (bambu hariç) oluşan, her katın kalınlığı 6 mm'yi geçmeyen diğer kontrplak: En az bir dış katı tropikal ahşaptan olan: Diğer: Diğer. Genel vergi oranı ad valorem olarak yüzde 8 olacaktır.

Prof. Dr. Nurcholish Madjid No. 159  Jombang 61419  Indonesia  RE:      The tariff classification of multilayer wood flooring from Indonesia  Dear Mr. Wang:  In your letter, dated July 17, 2026, you requested a binding tariff classification ruling on multilayer wood  flooring.  Product information and images were submitted for our review.  The products under consideration are three styles of engineered – or multilayer – wood flooring.  Style 1 is constructed of multiple layers of Mahogany, Gmelina, or Acacia, all tropical woods.  The image  shows seven layers of wood and you indicate that no layer exceeds 2.5mm in thickness.  The grain of each  layer runs at a 90-degree angle to the adjacent layers.  The panel is overlaid with a layer of  melamine-impregnated, printed paper, and the back of the panel is covered with irradiated cross-linked  polyethylene (IXPE) foam.  Both the paper and the foam obscure the grain of the wood beneath.  The panels  are tongued and grooved on their edges and ends.   Style 2 is constructed of multiple layers of Mahogany, Gmelina, or Acacia, all tropical woods.  The image  shows eight layers of wood and you indicate that no layer exceeds 2.5mm in thickness.  The grain of each  layer runs at a 90-degree angle to the adjacent layers.  The panel is overlaid with a layer of  melamine-impregnated, printed paper.  The paper obscures the grain of the wood beneath.  The panels are  tongued and grooved on their edges and ends.  Style 3 is constructed of multiple layers of Mahogany, Gmelina, or Acacia, all tropical woods.  The image  shows eight layers of wood and you indicate that no layer exceeds 2.5mm in thickness.  The grain of each  layer runs at a 90-degree angle to the adjacent layers.  The panel is overlaid with a layer of high-density  fiberboard (HDF) and is covered with a layer of melamine-impregnated, printed paper.  The paper and HDF  obscure the grain of the wood beneath.  The panels are tongued and grooved on their edges and ends.  

You suggest that Style 1 is classifiable under subheading 4412.91.5205 or 4418.99.9105, Harmonized Tariff  Schedule of the United States (HTSUS).  You suggest that Style 2 is classifiable under 4412.31.4875,  HTSUS.  You suggest that Style 3 is classifiable under 4412.91.5205, HTSUS.  We disagree with each of  these suggested classifications.  Subheading 4412.91.5205, HTSUS, provides for veneered panels that are not  plywood; we will show herein that the panels are each of a plywood construction.  Subheading 4418.99.9105,  HTSUS, provides for single layer wood flooring products that are not classified elsewhere.  The instant  panels are multilayer.  Subheading 4412.31.4875, HTSUS, provides for tropical plywood that is not surface  covered or surface covered with a clear or transparent material.  All of the panels are surface covered with  opaque materials.  As such, all of the suggested classifications are precluded.   The Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System Explanatory Notes (ENs) constitute the official  interpretation of the Harmonized System at the international level (for the 4-digit headings and the 6-digit  subheadings) and facilitate classification under the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States  (HTSUS) by offering guidance in understanding the scope of the headings and the General Rules of  Interpretation (GRIs). While neither legally binding nor dispositive of classification issues, the ENs provide  commentary on the scope of each heading of the HTSUS and are generally indicative of the proper  interpretation of the headings. See T.D. 89-80, 54 Fed. Reg. 35127-28 (Aug. 23, 1989).  The instant multilayer flooring panels meet the definition of “plywood” as set forth in the ENs to heading  4412. “Plywood” is defined as a panel “consisting of three or more sheets of wood glued and pressed one on  the other and generally disposed so that the grains of successive layers are at an angle.”  Plywood is  specifically provided for in heading 4412, HTSUS.   Note 4 to Chapter 44, HTSUS, sets for that goods of heading 4412 may be tongued and grooved:  Products of heading 4410, 4411 or 4412 may be worked to form the shapes provided for in respect of  the articles of heading 4409, curved, corrugated, perforated, cut or formed to shapes other than square  or rectangular or submitted to any other operation provided it does not give them the character of  articles of other headings.  Also, the language of subheadings within heading 4412 indicate that the panels may be surface covered.     Additional US Note 1(c) to Chapter 44, HTSUS, defines surface covering:  The term "surface covered," as applied to the articles of headings 4411 and 4412, means that one or  more exterior surfaces of a product have been treated with creosote or other wood preservatives, or  with fillers, sealers, waxes, oils, stains, varnishes, paints or enamels, or have been overlaid with paper,  fabric, plastics, base metal, or other material.  Each of the panels is surface covered with melamine-impregnated, printed paper.  Style 3 is also surface  covered with HDF.  The paper and HDF are both opaque surface coverings that obscure the grain of the  wood beneath, therefore, they do not meet the term “Not surface covered, or surface covered with a clear or  transparent material which does not obscure the grain, texture or markings of the face ply.”  Each product is  plywood with a surface covering that obscures the grain of the face ply.   The applicable subheading for Styles 1, 2, and 3 when there is an outer ply of Mahogany wood will be  4412.31.6100, HTSUS, which provides for Plywood, veneered panels and similar laminated wood: Other  plywood consisting solely of sheets of wood (other than  bamboo), each ply not exceeding 6mm in thickness:  With at least one outer ply of tropical wood: Other: With at least one outer ply of the following tropical  woods: Dark Red Meranti, Light Red Meranti, White Lauan, Sipo, Limba, Okoumé, Obeche, Acajou  d'Afrique, Sapelli, Virola, Mahogany, Palissandre de Para,  Palissandre de Rio or Palissandre de Rose.  The  general rate of duty will be 8 percent ad valorem. 

The applicable subheading for Styles 1, 2, and 3 when there is a face ply of Gmelina or Acacia will be  4412.31.9200, HTSUS, which provides for Plywood, veneered panels and similar laminated wood: Other  plywood consisting solely of sheets of wood (other than bamboo), each ply not exceeding 6mm in thickness:  With at least one outer ply of tropical wood: Other: Other.  The general rate of duty will be 8 percent ad  valorem.  This ruling does not address the applicability of any additional duties, taxes, fees, exactions and/or other  charges, which may apply to the goods discussed herein.  This includes, but is not limited to, tariffs and other  duties as provided for in Subchapter III to Chapter 99, HTSUS.  Thus, for example, in addition to the  classification stated above, the merchandise covered by this ruling may also need to be reported with either  the Chapter 99 provision under which an additional tariff applies or one of the Chapter 99 provisions  covering exceptions to such tariffs.
