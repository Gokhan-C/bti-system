The tariff classification of an electric heating pad from China

Eşyanın Tanımı (Türkçe özet)

2 Haziran 2026 tarihli yazınızda tarife sınıflandırma kararı talep etmiştiniz. Ürünün bir örneği bu ofise iletildi ve eğitim amacıyla saklanacak. "Isıtma Yastığı" olarak tanımlanan örnek, kullanıcının omuzlarına, boynuna ve sırtına takılmak üzere tasarlanmış bir Berkshire® elektrikli ısıtma yastığıdır ve gergin kasları gevşetmek ve sıcaklığı sağlamak için ısı terapisi sağlamayı amaçlamaktadır. Isıtma yastığı, aşağıdakilerden oluşan dört katmandan oluşur: yüzde 100 polyester örme peluş, boyalı kumaşlardan oluşan üst ve alt dış kabuk; yüzde 100 polyester dokunmamış iğneyle delinmiş kumaştan ikinci bir katman; ve ısıtma teline bağlanan yüzde 100 polyester dokunmamış iğneyle delinmiş kumaştan üçüncü bir katman. Düz bir şekilde döşendiğinde, pelerin benzeri ısıtma yastığının genişliği 26 inç ve uzunluğu 34 inçtir. Giyildiğinde, omuzlar boyunca 23 inç ve yükseklikte 28 inç ölçülerinde, konturlu bir uyum sağlar. Eşyayı omuzların etrafında sabitlemek için ön orta kısımda iki set çıtçıt düğme ve her iki tarafa dikilmiş, karın boyunca bağlanacak şekilde tasarlanmış 1 ½ inç genişliğinde ve 23 inç uzunluğunda kendi kumaşından bir kayış bulunur. Isıtma yastığı, kısmen yakanın kenarları ve yastığın alt kenarı boyunca birleştirilen cam boncuklarla yerinde tutulur. Isıtma yastığı ayrıca, iki saatlik otomatik kapanma güvenlik varsayılan ayarıyla birlikte on sıcaklık ayarına ve bir ila üç saat arasında değişen üç zamanlayıcı seçeneğine sahip çıkarılabilir bir el tipi denetleyiciye sahiptir. Isıtma yastığının, "evsel amaçlarla kullanılan türden diğer elektrotermik cihazlar, diğer elektrotermik cihazlar, diğerleri"ni öngören Amerika Birleşik Devletleri Uyumlaştırılmış Tarife Çizelgesi'nin (HTSUS) 8516.79.0000 alt pozisyonu altında sınıflandırılması gerektiğini önerdiniz. TARIFF NO.: 6307.90.9891
  Ms. Nicole Robertson  Berkshire Blanket  446 Main Street  Worcester, MA 01608  RE:  The tariff classification of an electric heating pad from China  Dear Ms. Robertson:  In your letter dated June 2, 2026, you requested a tariff classification ruling.  A sample of the product was  provided to this office and will be retained for training purposes.  The sample, identified as a “Heating Pad” is a Berkshire® electric heating pad designed to be worn over the  wearer’s shoulders, neck, and back and is intended to provide heat therapy to relax tight muscles and warmth.  The heating pad is composed of four layers consisting of: a top and bottom outer shell of 100 percent  polyester knitted plush, dyed, fabrics; a second layer of 100 percent polyester nonwoven needle-punched  fabric; and a third layer of 100 percent polyester nonwoven needle-punched fabric bonded to heating wire.    When laid flat, the cape-like heating pad measures 26 inches in width and 34 inches in length.  When worn, it  provides a contoured fit measuring 23 inches across the shoulders and 28 inches in height.  It features two  sets of snap buttons in the center front to secure the item around the shoulders and a 1 ½ inch wide by  23-inch-long self-fabric strap sewn to either side designed to be tied across the abdomen.  The heating pad is  kept in place by glass beads partially incorporated along the edges of the collar and the bottom edge of the  pad.  The heating pad also features a detachable handheld controller with ten temperature settings and three  timer options ranging from one to three hours, with a two-hour auto-off safety default setting.  You have suggested that the heating pad should be classified under subheading 8516.79.0000, Harmonized  Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS), which provides for "other electrothermic appliances of a kind  used for domestic purposes, other electrothermic appliances, other."  We disagree.  Our review of the sample  and the information in your submission, including subsequent email correspondence, suggests that the  heating pad is designed to be worn on or around the person.  Note 1(a) to Chapter 85 states: "This chapter  does not cover: Electrically warmed blankets, bed pads, foot-muffs or the like; electrically warmed clothing,  footwear or ear pads or other electrically warmed articles worn on or about the person." Therefore,  classification under subheading 8516.79.0000 is precluded. 

The applicable subheading for the “Heating Pad” will be 6307.90.9891, HTSUS, which provides for “Other  made-up articles, including dress patterns: Other: Other: Other: Other: Other.” The rate of duty will be 7  percent ad valorem.  The duties cited above are current as of this ruling’s issuance.  Duty rates are provided for your convenience  and are subject to change. The text of the most recent HTSUS and the accompanying duty rates are provided  at https://hts.usitc.gov/.  This ruling does not address the applicability of any additional duties, taxes, fees, exactions and/or other  charges, which may apply to the goods discussed herein.  This includes, but is not limited to, tariffs and other  duties as provided for in Subchapter III to Chapter 99, HTSUS.  Thus, for example, in addition to the  classification stated above, the merchandise covered by this ruling may also need to be reported with either  the Chapter 99 provision under which an additional tariff applies or one of the Chapter 99 provisions  covering exceptions to such tariffs.  For further information to assist with the importation process, please refer to the frequently updated Cargo  Systems Messaging Service (CSMS) messages at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/automated/cargo-systems-messaging-service and the Trade Remedies page at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/programs-administration/trade-remedies.  The holding set forth above applies only to the specific factual situation and merchandise description as  identified in the ruling request.  This position is clearly set forth in Title 19, Code of Federal Regulations  (CFR), Section 177.9(b)(1).  This section states that a ruling letter is issued on the assumption that all of the  information furnished in the ruling letter, whether directly, by reference, or by implication, is accurate and  complete in every material respect.  In the event that the facts are modified in any way, or if the goods do not  conform to these facts at time of importation, you should bring this to the attention of U.S. Customs and  Border Protection (CBP) and submit a request for a new ruling in accordance with 19 CFR 177.2.   Additionally, we note that the material facts described in the foregoing ruling may be subject to periodic  verification by CBP.  This ruling is being issued under the provisions of Part 177 of the Customs and Border Protection  Regulations (19 C.F.R. 177).  A copy of the ruling or the control number indicated above should be provided with the entry documents  filed at the time this merchandise is imported.
