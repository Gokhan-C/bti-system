Konu

The tariff classification of incomplete trailers from Canada

Eşyanın Tanımı (Türkçe özet)

2 Haziran 2026 tarihli yazınızda Kinetic Custom Trailers adına tarife sınıflandırma kararı talep etmişsiniz. Söz konusu öğenin iki (2) tamamlanmamış römork olduğu belirlendi. İlk parçanın Kinetik Yüksek Kapasiteli “CTU” Treyler olduğu belirlendi. Kinetic treyler, petrol kuyusu sahasında petrol kuyusu müdahalesi ve bakım hizmetleri sağlamak için son derece özel ekipmanlarla donatılacak, amaca yönelik olarak üretilmiş bir treyler şasisidir. Bu uygulamanın bir örneğinin petrol ve gaz kuyusu geliştirmede kullanılan kangal boru römorku olabileceğini belirtiyorsunuz. Bu tip römork, petrol ve gaz kuyusu işletme sahasında, ağırlıklı olarak petrol ve gaz sahası operasyonları için çeşitli amaçlarla kuyulara yerleştirilen 30.000 feet'ten fazla sürekli çelik boruyu tutmak için kullanılır. Kinetic amaca yönelik üretilmiştir ve ithalat sonrasında, müşteri tarafından belirlenen ekipman kalıcı olarak kurulana kadar müşterinin amaçlanan saha servis uygulaması için "kullanıma hazır" durumda değildir. İkinci öğe, Kinetik Özel Ekipman Montajlı "Basamak güvertesi" Şasisi olarak tanımlandı. Basamaklı şasi, önceden delinmiş montaj delikleri, özel tasarlanmış çapraz elemanlar veya montaj parçaları ve müşterinin ekipmanını kabul etmek için özel olarak tasarlanmış montaj braketleri içeren, çıplak boyalı bir şasidir. Yukarıda açıklanan Kinetik Yüksek Kapasiteli CTU treyleri ile karşılaştırıldığında bu, daha az karmaşık bir ekipman montajlı treylerdir. Bu tip römorkun yaygın bir kullanımı, güç üretmek veya doğrudan bir pompayı çalıştırmak için doğal gazlı yanmalı bir motor ve jeneratörle donatılmasıdır. Bu tip treyler tipik olarak aynı ünitelerin birçoğuyla, bazen bir kuyu sahasında veya kuyu lokasyonunda 15 üniteden fazla ile uyum içinde çalışır.

Sınıflandırmanın Gerekçesi (Türkçe özet)

Kinetik Yüksek Kapasiteli CTU ve Ekipman montajlı Step Deck römorklar için geçerli alt pozisyon, "Römorklar ve yarı römorklar; mekanik olarak tahrik edilmeyen diğer araçlar ve bunların parçaları: Diğer römorklar ve yarı römorklar" ifadesini öngören 8716.40.0000, HTSUS olacaktır. Genel vergi oranı Ücretsiz olacaktır.

  Mr. Nick Zosky  Dentons Cohen and Grigsby  625 Liberty Ave  Pittsburgh, PA 15222  RE:  The tariff classification of incomplete trailers from Canada  Dear Mr. Zosky:  In your letter dated June 2, 2026, you requested a tariff classification ruling, filed on behalf of Kinetic  Custom Trailers.  The item under consideration has been identified as two (2) unfinished trailers.  The first item has been identified as the Kinetic High Capacity “CTU” Trailer.  The Kinetic trailer is a  purpose-built trailer chassis that will be fitted with highly specialized equipment to provide oil well  intervention and maintenance services at the oil well site.  You state that one example of this application  would be a coil tubing trailer that is used for oil and gas well development.  This type of trailer is used at the  oil and gas well operation site to hold more than 30,000 feet of continuous steel tubing that is deployed into  wells for various purposes, predominantly for oil and gas field operations.  The Kinetic is purpose-built and,  upon importation, is not in a “ready to use” condition for the customer’s intended field-service application  until the customer-specified equipment is permanently installed.  The second item has been identified as the Kinetic Custom Equipment-Mounting “Step deck” Chassis.  The  step deck chassis is a bare painted chassis that includes pre-drilled mounting holes, custom designed  cross-members or mounts and mounting brackets that are designed specifically to accept a customer’s  equipment.  Compared to the Kinetic High Capacity CTU trailer described above, this is a less complex  equipment-mounting trailer.  A common usage of this type of trailer is that it is outfitted with a natural gas  combustion engine and generator to produce power or to directly drive a pump. This type of trailer would  typically operate in concert with many others of the same units, sometimes upwards of 15 units on a well site  or well location where it is used off highway in operation. Trailers of this type would operate on sites for  days or weeks at a time, if not longer, until they are redeployed to another site.  

A review of the information provided shows that there are no general transportation platforms, van type  enclosures, refrigeration equipment, ISO container twist-locks, container pins, standardized container  supports, or intermodal securement features installed on the trailers at time of manufacture or import, nor are  they equipped to transport ocean or intermodal containers used in interstate commerce to transport  merchandise across the United States.  This Kinetic trailer is approximately 14.5 ft wide and 72 ft long and, when connected with its ancillary jeep  booster and tractor, will have a gross vehicle weight of up to 400,000 lb.  In your request you suggest classification of the Kinetic trailers in 8716.40, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of  the United States (HTSUS), which provides for, “Trailers and semi-trailers; other vehicles, not mechanically  propelled; and parts thereof: Other trailers and semi-trailers.”  We agree.  The applicable subheading for the Kinetic High Capacity CTU and Equipment mounting Step Deck trailers  will be 8716.40.0000, HTSUS, which provides for “Trailers and semi-trailers; other vehicles, not  mechanically propelled; and parts thereof: Other trailers and semi-trailers.”  The general rate of duty will be  Free.  The duties cited above are current as of this ruling’s issuance.  Duty rates are provided for your convenience  and are subject to change. The text of the most recent HTSUS and the accompanying duty rates are provided  at https://hts.usitc.gov/.  This ruling does not address the applicability of any additional duties, taxes, fees, exactions and/or other  charges, which may apply to the goods discussed herein.  This includes, but is not limited to, tariffs and other  duties as provided for in Subchapter III to Chapter 99, HTSUS.  Thus, for example, in addition to the  classification stated above, the merchandise covered by this ruling may also need to be reported with either  the Chapter 99 provision under which an additional tariff applies or one of the Chapter 99 provisions  covering exceptions to such tariffs.  For further information to assist with the importation process, please refer to the frequently updated Cargo  Systems Messaging Service (CSMS) messages at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/automated/cargo-systems-messaging-service and the Trade Remedies page at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/programs-administration/trade-remedies.  The holding set forth above applies only to the specific factual situation and merchandise description as  identified in the ruling request.  This position is clearly set forth in Title 19, Code of Federal Regulations  (CFR), Section 177.9(b)(1).  This section states that a ruling letter is issued on the assumption that all of the  information furnished in the ruling letter, whether directly, by reference, or by implication, is accurate and  complete in every material respect.  In the event that the facts are modified in any way, or if the goods do not  conform to these facts at time of importation, you should bring this to the attention of U.S. Customs and  Border Protection (CBP) and submit a request for a new ruling in accordance with 19 CFR 177.2. This ruling is being issued under the provisions of Part 177 of the Customs and Border Protection  Regulations (19 C.F.R. 177).
