Konu

The tariff classification of Fosfomycin Tromethamine for oral solution in dosage form, from Mexico

Eşyanın Tanımı (Türkçe özet)

8 Temmuz 2026 tarihli yazınızda tarife sınıflandırma kararı talep etmiştiniz. Oral solüsyon için 3 gramlık granüller halinde ithal edilen Fosfomisin Trometamin, sentetik geniş spektrumlu bir antibiyotiktir. Sadece kadınlarda Escherichia coli ve Enterococcus faecalis'in duyarlı suşlarına bağlı komplike olmayan idrar yolu enfeksiyonlarının (akut sistit) tedavisinde endikedir.

Sınıflandırmanın Gerekçesi (Türkçe özet)

Dozaj formundaki oral çözelti için Fosfomisin Trometamin için geçerli alt pozisyon, Amerika Birleşik Devletleri Harmonize Tarife Tablosu (HTSUS) 3004.20.0085 olacaktır; bu listede "Terapötik veya profilaktik kullanımlar için karışık veya karışmamış ürünlerden oluşan, ölçülü dozlarda veya perakende satış için form veya ambalajlarda sunulan ilaçlar: Diğer, antibiyotik içerenler: Diğer: Diğer: Diğer." Genel vergi oranı ücretsiz olacaktır.

  Ciara Horgan  Northstar Healthcare UC  3300 Cork Airport Business Park  Cork T12XN72  Ireland  RE:  The tariff classification of Fosfomycin Tromethamine for oral solution in dosage form, from Mexico  Dear Ms. Horgan:  In your letter dated July 8, 2026, you requested a tariff classification ruling.  Fosfomycin Tromethamine, imported in 3 gram granules for oral solution, is a synthetic broad-spectrum  antibiotic. It is only indicated for the treatment of uncomplicated urinary tract infections (acute cystitis) in  women due to susceptible strains of Escherichia coli and Enterococcus faecalis.  The applicable subheading for the Fosfomycin Tromethamine for oral solution in dosage form will be  3004.20.0085, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS), which provides for “Medicaments  … consisting of mixed or unmixed products for therapeutic or prophylactic uses, put up in measured doses …  or in forms or packings for retail sale: Other, containing antibiotics: Other: Other: Other.” The general rate of  duty will be free.  The duties cited above are current as of this ruling’s issuance.  Duty rates are provided for your convenience  and are subject to change. The text of the most recent HTSUS and the accompanying duty rates are provided  at https://hts.usitc.gov/.  This merchandise may be subject to the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act and/or The Public Health  Security and Bioterrorism Preparedness and Response Act of 2002 (The Bioterrorism Act), which are  administered by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA). Information on the Federal Food, Drug, and  Cosmetic Act, as well as The Bioterrorism Act, can be obtained by calling the FDA at 1-888-463-6332, or by  visiting their website at www.fda.gov.  This ruling does not address the applicability of any additional duties, taxes, fees, exactions and/or other  charges, which may apply to the goods discussed herein. duties as provided for in Subchapter III to Chapter 99, HTSUS.
