Konu

The tariff classification of a sofa sectional storage console from Vietnam

Eşyanın Tanımı (Türkçe özet)

10 Haziran 2026 tarihli mektubunuzda, Kuka (HK) Trade Co. Ltd. adına tarife sınıflandırma kararı talep etmişsiniz. İnceleme için numuneler, teknik ve açıklayıcı literatür, ürün açıklaması ve üretim özeti sunulmuş. Ürün 1987687, "Gizli Bardak Tutuculu Deri Saklama Konsolu" modüler bir kanepe kesit ünitesidir. Döşemeli kanepe kesit ünitesi, ahşap bir çerçeveye tutturulmuş deri, köpük, polyester elyaf ve polivinil klorürden (PVC) yapılmıştır. Ahşap çerçeve, (1) kontrplak, (2) lamine kaplama kereste (LVL) ve Güney çamı ağacını içeren işlenmiş ahşap malzemelerden oluşur. Modüler kanepe ünitesinde USB-A ve USB-C şarj bağlantı noktaları ve bir çift bardak tutucunun bulunduğu bir saklama bölmesi bulunacak. Modüler kanepe kesitli oturma ünitesi, ilave kanepe kesit ünitelerine (ayrıca satılır) entegrasyon ve montaj için bağlantı direkleri ve/veya bağlantı klipsleri şeklinde montaj donanımı içerir. Modüler kanepe kesit ünitesi boyutları yaklaşık olarak 40,5" uzunluğunda, 12,5" genişliğinde ve 26,3" yüksekliğindedir. Karar talebi, söz konusu malın Amerika Birleşik Devletleri Harmonize Tarife Çizelgesi'nin (HTSUS) 9403.60.8093 alt pozisyonunda sınıflandırılmasını istemektedir. Katılmıyoruz. Harmonize Mal Tanımı ve Kodlama Sistemi Açıklayıcı Notları (EN'ler), Harmonize Sistemin resmi yorumunu oluşturmaktadır. Uluslararası düzeyde EN I, Genel Yorum Kuralı (GRI) 2(a) şunu öngörmektedir: “Kural 2(a)'nın ilk bölümü, belirli bir eşyaya atıfta bulunan herhangi bir başlığın kapsamını, sadece tam eşyayı değil aynı zamanda o tamamlanmamış veya bitmemiş eşyayı da kapsayacak şekilde genişletir; ancak, sunulduğu şekliyle tamamlanmış veya tamamlanmış temel karaktere sahiptir.

Sınıflandırmanın Gerekçesi (Türkçe özet)

Söz konusu eşya için geçerli alt pozisyon, "Yatağa dönüştürülebilen olsun olmasın (9402 pozisyonundakiler hariç) Koltuklar ve bunların parçaları: Ahşap çerçeveli diğer koltuklar: Döşemeli: Diğer: Diğer ev eşyaları" ifadesini öngören 9401.61.6011, HTSUS olacaktır. Genel vergi oranı ücretsiz olacaktır.

  Melissa Birch  Radiant World Trade Services  1300 Mendota Heights Rd  Mendota Heights, MN 55120  RE:  The tariff classification of a sofa sectional storage console from Vietnam  Dear Ms. Birch:  In your letter dated June 10, 2026, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of Kuka (HK) Trade  Co. Ltd.  In lieu of samples, technical and illustrative literature, a product description, and a manufacturing  synopsis were provided for review.  Item 1987687, the “Leather Storage Console with Hidden Cupholders,” is a modular sofa sectional unit.   The  upholstered sofa sectional unit is constructed of leather, foam, polyester fiber, and polyvinyl chloride (PVC)  that is affixed to a wooden frame.  The wooden frame consists of engineered wood materials that include (1)  plywood, (2) laminated veneer lumber (LVL) and Southern pine wood.  The modular sofa sectional unit will  have USB-A and USB-C charging ports and a storage compartment with a pair of cupholders.  The modular  sofa sectional seating unit contains assembly hardware in the form of connection posts and/or connection  clips for integration and assembly onto additional sofa sectional units (sold separately).  The modular sofa  sectional unit dimensions are approximately 40.5" in length, 12.5" in width, and 26.3" in height.   The ruling request seeks classification of the subject merchandise in subheading 9403.60.8093 of the  Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS).  We disagree.  The Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System Explanatory Notes (ENs) constitutes the  official interpretation of the Harmonized System at the international level.  EN I to General Rule of Interpretation (GRI) 2(a) provides: “the first part of Rule 2(a) extends the scope of  any heading which refers to a particular article to cover not only the complete article but also that article  incomplete or unfinished, provided that, as presented, it has the essential character of the complete or finished  article.” 

EN V to GRI 2(a) provides:  “the complete or finished articles presented unassembled or disassembled are to  be classified in the same heading as the assembled article.  When goods are presented, it is usually for  reasons such as requirements or convenience of packing, handling or transport.”  The “Leather Storage Console with Hidden Cupholders” at issue, though incomplete without being  connected, is nevertheless dedicated to a sofa sectional.  The sofa sectional component does not have any  commercial reality apart from being connected into an assembled sofa.   Chapter 94, Legal Note 2, 2(a), and 2(b) provides: “articles (other than parts) referred to in headings 9401 to  9403 are to be classified in those headings only if they are designed for placing on the floor or ground.  The  following are, however, to be classified in the above-mentioned headings even if they are designed to be  hung, to be fixed to the wall or to stand one on the other:   2(a) Cupboards, bookcases, other shelved furniture (including single shelves presented   with supports  for fixing them to the wall) and unit furniture.  2(b) Seats and Beds.”   The “Leather Storage Console with Hidden Cupholders” is designed to be placed on the floor or ground, is  designed to be placed side-by-side additional sofa sectional components and is designed to be configured or  reconfigured within a multi-piece sofa sectional.  The “Leather Storage Console with Hidden Cupholders”  falls within the construct of Chapter 94, Legal Note 2, 2(a), and 2(b).  Further, the ENs to Chapter 94, heading 9401 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule (HTS), states: “lounge  chairs, arm-chairs, folding chairs, desk chairs, infants’ high chairs and children’s seats designed to be hung  on the back of other seats (including vehicle seats), grandfather chairs, benches, couches (including those  with electrical heating, settees, sofas, ottomans and the like, stools (such as piano stools, draughts men’s  stools, typists’ stools, and dual purpose stool-steps), seats which incorporate a sound system and are suitable  for use with video game consoles and machines, television or satellite receivers, as well as with DVD, music  CD, MP3 or video cassette players” as exemplars for seats classified in heading 9401, HTS.    “Seats of this heading may incorporate complementary non-seat components, for example, toy components, a  vibration function, music or sound players, as well as lighting features.”  The “Leather Storage Console with Hidden Cupholders” is within the construct of the ENs to heading 9401  HTS for seats.  The upholstered sofa sectional unit is a dedicated seating component that will be integrated  within a line of sofa sectionals.  The connection posts and/or connection clips will allow other sofa sectional  seating components to be interlinked and create different modular seating configurations.     The applicable subheading for the subject merchandise will be 9401.61.6011, HTSUS, which provides for  “Seats (other than those of heading 9402), whether or not convertible into beds, and parts thereof:  Other  seats, with wooden frames:  Upholstered:  Other:  Other household.”  The general rate of duty will be free.  The duties cited above are current as of this ruling’s issuance.  Duty rates are provided for your convenience  and are subject to change. The text of the most recent HTSUS and the accompanying duty rates are provided  at https://hts.usitc.gov/.  This ruling does not address the applicability of any additional duties, taxes, fees, exactions and/or other  charges, which may apply to the goods discussed herein.  This includes, but is not limited to, tariffs and other  duties as provided for in Subchapter III to Chapter 99, HTSUS.  Thus, for example, in addition to the  classification stated above, the merchandise covered by this ruling may also need to be reported with either  the Chapter 99 provision under which an additional tariff applies or one of the Chapter 99 provisions  covering exceptions to such tariffs.

The holding set forth above applies only to the specific factual situation and merchandise description as  identified in the ruling request.  This position is clearly set forth in Title 19, Code of Federal Regulations  (CFR), Section 177.9(b)(1).  This section states that a ruling letter is issued on the assumption that all of the  information furnished in the ruling letter, whether directly, by reference, or by implication, is accurate and  complete in every material respect.  In the event that the facts are modified in any way, or if the goods do not  conform to these facts at time of importation, you should bring this to the attention of U.S. Customs and  Border Protection (CBP) and submit a request for a new ruling in accordance with 19 CFR 177.2.
