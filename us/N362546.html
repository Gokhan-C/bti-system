Konu

The tariff classification of a vehicle lift, an automated mobile robot, and a pallet buffer from Israel

Eşyanın Tanımı (Türkçe özet)

24 Haziran 2026 tarihli mektubunuzda, müvekkiliniz Five Points Development Group adına tarife sınıflandırma kararı talep etmişsiniz. Söz konusu ürün, bir araç asansörü, iki otomatik mobil robot, bir palet tampon makinesi, giriş odası bileşenleri, şarj cihazları, elektrik dolapları ve diğer bileşenleri içerecek bir otomatik park sistemi olarak adlandırılıyor. Bu bileşenler tek bir yapısal çerçeve, ortak taban veya ortak konutla sınırlı kalmayacak, bir konut binasının tamamında farklı katlara ve ayrı alanlara kurulacaktır. Araç asansörü, katlar arasında bir paleti (araçlı veya araçsız) kaldırmak için tasarlanmıştır. Bir binanın altyapısına dahil edilmiştir ve raylarla donatılmış dikey bir çerçeveye tutturulmuş bir platform, bir karşı ağırlık, bir redüktörlü motor, bir dengeleme tertibatı, dişliler ve kaldırma zincirlerinden oluşur. Asansör, elektrik motoruyla çalıştırılıyor, 5.100 kilogram yük kapasitesine sahip ve dakikada 15 metre hızla hareket ediyor. Otomatik mobil robot, bir paleti (araçlı veya araçsız) özel bir park yerine taşımak için tasarlanmıştır. Yaklaşık 3.650 milimetre uzunluğunda, 1.850 milimetre genişliğinde ve 340 milimetre yüksekliğinde dikdörtgen şasili, kendinden tahrikli, pille çalışan bir ünitedir. Ana bileşenleri arasında vidalı kriko tahrik ünitesi tertibatı, bir elektrik ünitesi, tekerlek tertibatları ve sensörler bulunur. Robot, vidalı kriko tahrik ünitesi tertibatı tarafından tahrik edilen dört vidalı krikodan oluşan bir kaldırma mekanizmasıyla donatılmıştır. Bu mekanizma 110 milimetrelik bir kaldırma strokuna sahiptir. Palet tamponu, boş paletleri istifleyen ve depolayan bir makinedir. Dört ayarlanabilir sütunla desteklenen iki mekanik kaldırma çerçevesi düzeneğinden oluşur. Makine hidroliktir

Sınıflandırmanın Gerekçesi (Türkçe özet)

Araç kaldırma ve palet tamponu için geçerli alt pozisyon, "Diğer kaldırma, taşıma, yükleme veya boşaltma makineleri (örneğin asansörler, yürüyen merdivenler, konveyörler, teleferikler): Diğer makineler: Diğer" ifadesini öngören 8428.90.0390, HTSUS olacaktır. Genel vergi oranı ücretsiz olacaktır. Otomatik mobil robot için geçerli alt başlık 8427.10.8050, HTSUS olacaktır ve bu başlık şu ifadeyi içerir: "Forkliftler; kaldırma veya taşıma ekipmanıyla donatılmış diğer iş kamyonları: Elektrik motoruyla çalışan kendinden tahrikli kamyonlar: Diğer: Otomatik yönlendirmeli araç (AGV): Diğer." Genel vergi oranı ücretsiz olacaktır. Metal paletleri, şarj cihazlarını, yapısal kapıları, giriş odası bileşenlerini, elektrik kabinini ve diğer bileşenleri sınıflandırmak için yeterli bilgi yoktur. 1 Temmuz 2026'daki toplantımıza göre bu konuların ayrı ayrı ele alınması gerekecek. Görev oranları size kolaylık sağlamak amacıyla verilmiştir ve değişebilir. En güncel HTSUS metni ve beraberindeki vergi oranları https://hts.usitc.gov/ adresinde sunulmaktadır.

  Gail Cumins  Sharretts Paley Carter & Blauvelt PC  75 Broad Street  New York, NY 10004  RE:  The tariff classification of a vehicle lift, an automated mobile robot, and a pallet buffer from Israel  Dear Ms. Cumins:  In your letter dated June 24, 2026, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, Five  Points Development Group.  The merchandise under consideration is referred to as an automated parking system that will include a  vehicle lift, two automated mobile robots, a pallet buffer machine, entry room components, chargers,  electrical cabinets, and other components. These components will not be confined to a single structural  frame, common base, or common housing, but are installed throughout a residential building on different  floors and in separate areas.  The vehicle lift is designed to lift a pallet (with or without a vehicle) between floors. It is incorporated into a  building’s infrastructure and consists of a platform affixed to a vertical frame fitted with rails, a  counterweight, a gearmotor, a leveling assembly, sprockets, and lifting chains. The lift is driven by an electric  motor, has a 5,100-kilogram load capacity, and moves at 15 meters per minute.  The automated mobile robot is designed to transport a pallet (with or without a vehicle) to a dedicated  parking spot. It is a self-propelled, battery-powered unit with a rectangular chassis that measures  approximately 3,650 millimeters in length, 1,850 millimeters in width, and 340 millimeters in height. Its  main components include a screw jack drive unit assembly, an electrical unit, wheel assemblies, and sensors.  The robot is fitted with a lifting mechanism, which consists of four screw jacks that are driven by the screw  jack drive unit assembly. This mechanism has a 110-millimeter lifting stroke.  The pallet buffer is a machine that stacks and stores empty pallets. It consists of two mechanical lifting frame  assemblies supported by four adjustable columns. The machine is hydraulically powered and designed to lift  up to five pallets. It has a lifting stroke of 700 millimeters and a load capacity of 3,500 kilograms. 

In your submission, you suggest that the components, when imported in a single shipment, are classified as a  functional unit pursuant to Note 4 to Section XVI under heading 8428 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of  the United States (HTSUS), which provides for “Other lifting, handling, loading or unloading machinery (for  example, elevators, escalators, conveyors, teleferics).” We disagree. As noted above, the machines, entry  room, and structures are distributed across different floors and throughout the building. The automated  mobile robots are free-roaming independent machine robots. The pallet buffer is a stand-alone module that  functions independently of the other machines. We do not find that the separate components perform a single,  clearly defined function described by heading 8428. Rather, the various machines, components, and systems  serve multiple, distinct purposes or fulfill auxiliary functions. Therefore, each component will be separately  classified.  You cite New York ruling F82199 (February 14, 2000); New York ruling I87239 (October 31, 2002); New  York ruling N022821 (February 19, 2008); and New York ruling 805706 (January 31, 1995), in support of  classifying the subject system in heading 8428. We find that the machines in the cited rulings are  distinguishable from the subject goods. These rulings involved self-contained lifting equipment within a  single structure or frame.  The applicable subheading for the vehicle lift and pallet buffer will be 8428.90.0390, HTSUS, which  provides for “Other lifting, handling, loading or unloading machinery (for example, elevators, escalators,  conveyors, teleferics): Other machinery: Other.” The general rate of duty will be free.  The applicable subheading for the automated mobile robot will be 8427.10.8050, HTSUS, which provides for  “Fork-lift trucks; other works trucks fitted with lifting or handling equipment: Self-propelled trucks powered  by an electric motor: Other: Automated guided vehicle (AGV): Other.” The general rate of duty will be free.  There is insufficient information to classify the metal pallets, chargers, structural gates, entry room  components, electrical cabinet, and other components. As per our meeting on July 1, 2026, these items will  need to be addressed separately.  Duty rates are provided for your convenience and are subject to change. The text of the most recent HTSUS  and the accompanying duty rates are provided at https://hts.usitc.gov/.  This ruling does not address the applicability of any additional duties, taxes, fees, exactions and/or other  charges, which may apply to the goods discussed herein.  This includes, but is not limited to, tariffs and other  duties as provided for in Subchapter III to Chapter 99, HTSUS.  Thus, for example, in addition to the  classification stated above, the merchandise covered by this ruling may also need to be reported with either  the Chapter 99 provision under which an additional tariff applies or one of the Chapter 99 provisions  covering exceptions to such tariffs.  For further information to assist with the importation process, please refer to the frequently updated Cargo  Systems Messaging Service (CSMS) messages at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/automated/cargo-systems-messaging-service and the Trade Remedies page at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/programs-administration/trade-remedies.  The holding set forth above applies only to the specific factual situation and merchandise description as  identified in the ruling request.  This position is clearly set forth in Title 19, Code of Federal Regulations  (CFR), Section 177.9(b)(1).  This section states that a ruling letter is issued on the assumption that all of the  information furnished in the ruling letter, whether directly, by reference, or by implication, is accurate and  complete in every material respect.  In the event that the facts are modified in any way, or if the goods do not  conform to these facts at time of importation, you should bring this to the attention of U.S. Customs and  Border Protection (CBP) and submit a request for a new ruling in accordance with 19 CFR 177.2.   Additionally, we note that the material facts described in the foregoing ruling may be subject to periodic  verification by CBP. 

This ruling is being issued under the provisions of Part 177 of the Customs and Border Protection  Regulations (19 C.F.R. 177).  A copy of the ruling or the control number indicated above should be provided with the entry documents  filed at the time this merchandise is imported.
