Konu

The tariff classification of anti-friction sleeves from China

Eşyanın Tanımı (Türkçe özet)

3 Temmuz 2026 tarihli yazınızda tarife sınıflandırma kararı talep etmiştiniz. Söz konusu ürünler, parça numarası 600-P-1664 olan sabit rulman manşonu ve 600-P-1665 parça numarası olan döner rulman manşonu olarak tanımlanmaktadır. Her manşon, tungsten karbür plakalara sahip çelik bir yapısal gövdeden oluşur. Döner manşonu içeren sabit manşon, motor üzerinde sabit kalır. Dişli döner manşon, motor şaftı ile birlikte döner. Kullanım sırasında iki manşon, sürtünmeyi en aza indiren ve sıvının kuyu içi çamur motoru içinde akmasına izin veren bir düzenek olarak işlev görür. Bu manşonlar ayrı ayrı veya değişen miktarlarda ithal edilebilse de genellikle eşit miktarlarda birlikte ithal edilirler ve bu da 19 CFR §141.58'in uygulanmasını destekler. Manşonlar aynı miktarda ve aynı sevkiyatla birlikte ithal edildiğinde, manşon takımı için geçerli alt pozisyon, Transmisyon millerini (eksantrik milleri ve krank milleri dahil) ve krankları öngören Amerika Birleşik Devletleri Harmonize Tarife Tablosu (HTSUS) 8483.30.8065 olacaktır; rulman yatakları, yataklı yataklar ve kaymalı mil yatakları; dişliler ve dişliler; bilyalı veya makaralı vidalar; tork konvertörleri dahil dişli kutuları ve diğer hız değiştiriciler; kasnak blokları dahil volanlar ve kasnaklar; kavramalar ve şaft kaplinleri (üniversal mafsallar dahil); bunların parçaları: Rulman yatakları; düz mil yatakları: Diğer: Düz mil yatakları: Muhafazalı: Diğer. Genel vergi oranı değer üzerinden yüzde 4,5 olacaktır. Aksi takdirde,

  Jason Yang  Zigong International Marketing LLC  16504 Aldine Westfield Road, Building A  Houston, TX 77032  RE:  The tariff classification of anti-friction sleeves from China  Dear Mr. Yang:  In your letter dated July 3, 2026, you requested a tariff classification ruling.  The products at issue are identified as a stationary bearing sleeve, part number 600-P-1664, and a rotary  bearing sleeve, part number 600-P-1665. Each sleeve consists of a steel structural body that features tungsten  carbide tiles. The stationary sleeve, which incorporates the rotary sleeve, remains fixed on the motor. The  threaded rotary sleeve rotates together with the shaft of the motor.  In use, the two sleeves function as an  assembly that minimizes friction and allows for fluid to flow within a downhole mud motor. While these  sleeves may be imported separately or in varying quantities, they are generally imported together in equal  quantities, supporting the application of 19 CFR §141.58.  When the sleeves are imported together in the same quantity and the same shipments, the applicable  subheading for the sleeve assembly will be 8483.30.8065, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States  (HTSUS), which provides for Transmission shafts (including camshafts and crankshafts) and cranks; bearing  housings, housed bearings and plain shaft bearings; gears and gearing; ball or roller screws; gear boxes and  other speed changers, including torque converters; flywheels and pulleys, including pulley blocks; clutches  and shaft couplings (including universal joints); parts thereof: Bearing housings; plain shaft bearings: Other:  Plain shaft bearings: With housing: Other. The general rate of duty will be 4.5 percent ad valorem.  Otherwise, the applicable subheading for each sleeve will be 8483.90.3000, HTSUS, which provides for  Transmission shafts (including camshafts and crankshafts) and cranks; bearing housings, housed bearings and  plain shaft bearings; gears and gearing; ball or roller screws; gear boxes and other speed changers, including  torque converters; flywheels and pulleys, including pulley blocks; clutches and shaft couplings (including  universal joints); parts thereof: Toothed wheels, chain sprockets and other transmission elements presented  separately; parts: Parts of bearing housings and plain shaft bearings: Other. The general rate of duty will be  4.5 percent ad valorem. 

The duties cited above are current as of this ruling’s issuance. Duty rates are provided for your convenience  and are subject to change. The text of the most recent HTSUS and the accompanying duty rates are provided  at https://hts.usitc.gov/.  This ruling does not address the applicability of any additional duties, taxes, fees, exactions and/or other  charges, which may apply to the goods discussed herein.  This includes, but is not limited to, tariffs and other  duties as provided for in Subchapter III to Chapter 99, HTSUS.  Thus, for example, in addition to the  classification stated above, the merchandise covered by this ruling may also need to be reported with either  the Chapter 99 provision under which an additional tariff applies or one of the Chapter 99 provisions  covering exceptions to such tariffs.  For further information to assist with the importation process, please refer to the frequently updated Cargo  Systems Messaging Service (CSMS) messages at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/automated/cargo-systems-messaging-service and the Trade Remedies page at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/programs-administration/trade-remedies.
