Konu

The tariff classification of various models of the 2027 Kia EV3 electric vehicle from Mexico

Eşyanın Tanımı (Türkçe özet)

27 Temmuz 2026 tarihli yazınızda tarife sınıflandırma kararı talep etmiştiniz. Söz konusu ürün, 5 kapılı küçük bir spor arazi aracı (SUV) olan 2027 Kia EV3 akülü elektrikli araçtır. Talebinizde, Kia EV3'ün önden çekişli (FWD) ve dört tekerlekten çekişli (AWD) varyantlar dahil olmak üzere yedi farklı donanım seviyesinde, farklı akü kapasiteleri, motor çıkışları ve EPA dereceli sürüş menzilleriyle sunulacağını belirtmişsiniz. 2027 Kia EV3'ün tüm donanım seviyeleri, beş yolcuya kadar taşıyacak şekilde tasarlandı. Kargo kapasitesi 2. koltuk sırasının arkasında 26,1 fit küptür ve ikinci sıra katlandığında 56,5 fit küptür. Yolcu bölmesi hacmi 97,6 ila 99,7 fit küp arasında değişmektedir. İlk donanım seviyesi Light FWD olarak tanımlanır. Light FWD, 58,3 kWh lityum iyon polimer pil ve 261 mil menzile sahip 150 kW elektrik motoruyla donatılmıştır. İkinci ve üçüncü donanım seviyeleri Rüzgar FWD ve Kara FWD'dir. Wind and Land FWD, 81,4 kWh lityum iyon polimer piller ve 321 mil menzile sahip 150 kW elektrik motorlarıyla donatılmıştır. Dördüncü, beşinci ve altıncı donanım seviyeleri, tümü 195 kW çift motorlu güç aktarma organlarıyla donatılmış Wind AWD, Land AWD ve GT-Line AWD'dir. Yedinci ve son donanım seviyesi, 215 kW'lık çift motorlu güç aktarım mekanizmasıyla donatılmış GT AWD'dir. Tüm AWD donanımları 81,4 kWh lityum iyon polimer pil kullanır ve EPA dereceli 280 mil menzile sahiptir.

Sınıflandırmanın Gerekçesi (Türkçe özet)

2027 Kia EV3 Light FWD için geçerli alt başlık, 8703.80.0020, Amerika Birleşik Devletleri Uyumlaştırılmış Tarife Tablosu (HTSUS) olacaktır;

(87.02 pozisyonundakiler hariç) insan taşımak için tasarlanmış, steyşın vagonlar ve yarış arabaları dahil: Yalnızca elektrik tahrikli motorlara sahip diğer araçlar: EPA onaylı menzili 250 mili geçmeyen: Yeni. Genel vergi oranı değer üzerinden yüzde 2,5 olacaktır. Geriye kalan 2027 Kia EV3 donanım seviyeleri (Land FWD, Wind FWD, Wind AWD, Land AWD, GT-Line AWD ve GT AWD) için geçerli alt pozisyon 8703.80.0060, HTSUS olacaktır; bu, "Steyşın vagonlar ve yarış arabaları da dahil olmak üzere, esas olarak insan taşımak için tasarlanmış motorlu taşıtlar ve diğer motorlu taşıtlar (8702 pozisyonundakiler hariç): Yalnızca elektrikli motorlu diğer taşıtlar" hükmünü öngörmektedir. itiş gücü için: EPA dereceli menzili 250 mili aşan: Yeni.” Genel vergi oranı değer üzerinden yüzde 2,5 olacaktır.

  Mr. Jason Kwon  Kia America, Inc.  111 Peters Canyon Rd.  Irvine, CA  92606  RE:  The tariff classification of various models of the 2027 Kia EV3 electric vehicle from Mexico  Dear Mr. Kwon:  In your letter dated July 27, 2026, you requested a tariff classification ruling.  The item under consideration is the 2027 Kia EV3 battery electric vehicle, a 5-door small sport utility vehicle  (SUV).  In your request you state that the Kia EV3 will be offered in seven different trim levels, including  front-wheel drive (FWD) and all-wheel drive (AWD) variants, with differing battery capacities, motor  outputs, and EPA-rated driving ranges.  All trim levels for the 2027 Kia EV3 are designed to transport up to  five passengers.  Cargo capacity is 26.1 cubic feet behind the 2nd row of seats and up to 56.5 cubic feet with  the second row folded.  Passenger compartment volume ranges between 97.6 to 99.7 cubic feet.  The first trim level is identified as the Light FWD.  The Light FWD is equipped with a 58.3 kWh lithium-ion  polymer battery and a 150-kW electric motor with a range of 221 miles.  The second and third trim levels are the Wind FWD and the Land FWD.  The Wind and Land FWD are  equipped with 81.4 kWh lithium-ion polymer batteries and 150 kW electric motors with a range of 321 miles.  The fourth, fifth, and sixth trim levels are the Wind AWD, Land AWD, and GT-Line AWD which are all  equipped with 195 kW dual-motor powertrains.  The seventh and final trim level is the GT AWD which is equipped with a 215 kW dual-motor powertrain.   All AWD trims utilize an 81.4 kWh lithium-ion polymer battery and have an EPA-rated range of 280 miles.  The applicable subheading for the 2027 Kia EV3 Light FWD will be 8703.80.0020, Harmonized Tariff  Schedule of the United States (HTSUS), which provides for “Motor cars and other motor vehicles principally 

designed for the transport of persons (other than those of heading 8702), including station wagons and racing  cars: Other vehicles, with only electric motors for propulsion: With an EPA-rated range not exceeding 250  miles: New.”  The general rate of duty will be 2.5 percent ad valorem.  The applicable subheading for the remaining 2027 Kia EV3 trim levels (Land FWD, Wind FWD, Wind  AWD, Land AWD, GT-Line AWD, and GT AWD) will be 8703.80.0060, HTSUS, which provides for  “Motor cars and other motor vehicles principally designed for the transport of persons (other than those of  heading 8702), including station wagons and racing cars: Other vehicles, with only electric motors for  propulsion: With an EPA-rated range exceeding 250 miles: New.”  The general rate of duty will be 2.5  percent ad valorem.  The duties cited above are current as of this ruling’s issuance.  Duty rates are provided for your convenience  and are subject to change. The text of the most recent HTSUS and the accompanying duty rates are provided  at https://hts.usitc.gov/.  This ruling does not address the applicability of any additional duties, taxes, fees, exactions and/or other  charges, which may apply to the goods discussed herein.  This includes, but is not limited to, tariffs and other  duties as provided for in Subchapter III to Chapter 99, HTSUS.  Thus, for example, in addition to the  classification stated above, the merchandise covered by this ruling may also need to be reported with either  the Chapter 99 provision under which an additional tariff applies or one of the Chapter 99 provisions  covering exceptions to such tariffs.  For further information to assist with the importation process, please refer to the frequently updated Cargo  Systems Messaging Service (CSMS) messages at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/automated/cargo-systems-messaging-service and the Trade Remedies page at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/programs-administration/trade-remedies.  The holding set forth above applies only to the specific factual situation and merchandise description as  identified in the ruling request.  This position is clearly set forth in Title 19, Code of Federal Regulations  (CFR), Section 177.9(b)(1).  This section states that a ruling letter is issued on the assumption that all of the  information furnished in the ruling letter, whether directly, by reference, or by implication, is accurate and  complete in every material respect.  In the event that the facts are modified in any way, or if the goods do not  conform to these facts at time of importation, you should bring this to the attention of U.S. Customs and  Border Protection (CBP) and submit a request for a new ruling in accordance with 19 CFR 177.2.  Additionally, we note that the material facts described in the foregoing ruling may be subject to periodic  verification by CBP.  This ruling is being issued under the provisions of Part 177 of the Customs and Border Protection  Regulations (19 C.F.R. 177). 

A copy of the ruling or the control number indicated above should be provided with the entry documents  filed at the time this merchandise is imported.  If you have any questions regarding the ruling, please contact  National Import Specialist Matthew Sullivan at matthew.sullivan@cbp.dhs.gov.
