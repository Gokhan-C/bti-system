Konu

The tariff classification of a costume from China and Vietnam.

Eşyanın Tanımı (Türkçe özet)

21 Temmuz 2026 tarihli mektubunuzda, müşteriniz California Kostüm, Inc. adına bir tarife sınıflandırma kararı talep etmiştiniz. Olympian Goddess olarak tanımlanan, WMT5027-027 ürün numaralı, üzerine drapeli kolsuz bir kazak elbise, iki adet omuza takılan madalyon ve iki kol bandından oluşan yetişkinlere yönelik bir kostüm örneği gönderdiniz. Genel Yorum Kuralı 3(b), malların ilk bakışta iki veya daha fazla başlık altında sınıflandırılabilmesi ve perakende satışa yönelik setler halinde sunulması durumunda uygulanır. GRI 3(b) malların “kendilerine temel karakterini veren malzeme veya bileşenden oluşuyormuş gibi sınıflandırılacağını” belirtmektedir. İnsan yapımı ince örgü kumaş elbise, setin temel karakterini sağlıyor ve bir kez katlanan ve tek sıra gevşek, uzun düz dikişlerle dikilen boyun ve kol oyuklarındaki kenarlara sahip. Ekli örtü, arka bağlar ve etek ucunun işlenmemiş, tamamlanmamış kenarları vardır.

Sınıflandırmanın Gerekçesi (Türkçe özet)

Olimpiyat Tanrıçası kostümü için geçerli alt başlık, ürün numarası WMT5027-027, 9505.90.6000 olacaktır, Amerika Birleşik Devletleri Uyumlaştırılmış Tarife Çizelgesi (HTSUS), "Sihirbazlık numaraları ve pratik şaka malzemeleri de dahil olmak üzere şenlik, karnaval veya diğer eğlence eşyaları; bunların parça ve aksesuarları: Diğer: Diğer." Genel vergi oranı ücretsiz olacaktır.

  Mr. Frank Gomez  World Exchange, Inc.  7930 El Manor Ave  Los Angeles, CA 90045  RE:  The tariff classification of a costume from China and Vietnam.  Dear Mr. Gomez:  In your letter dated July 21, 2026, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client,  California Costume, Inc.  You submitted a sample of an adult size costume identified as Olympian Goddess, item number  WMT5027-027, which consists of a sleeveless pullover dress with attached drape, two pin-on shoulder  medallions and two armbands. General Rule of Interpretation 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie,  classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the  goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential  character.” The flimsy man-made knitted textile dress provides the essential character of the set and features   edges on the neck and armholes that are folded over once and sewn with a single line of loose, long straight  stitches. The attached drape, back ties, and hem bottom have raw, unfinished edges.  The applicable subheading for the Olympian Goddess costume, item number WMT5027-027 will be  9505.90.6000, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS), which provides for “Festive,  carnival or other entertainment articles, including magic tricks, and practical joke articles; parts and  accessories thereof: Other: Other.” The general rate of duty will be free.  The duties cited above are current as of this ruling’s issuance. Duty rates are provided for your convenience  and are subject to change. The text of the most recent HTSUS and the accompanying duty rates are provided  at https://hts.usitc.gov/.  Please note that separate Federal Trade Commission marking requirements exist regarding country of origin,  fiber content, and other information that must appear on many textile items. You should contact the Federal 

Trade Commission, Division of Enforcement, 600 Pennsylvania Avenue, N.W., Washington, D.C., 20580,  for information on the applicability of these requirements to this item. Information can also be found on the  FTC website www.ftc.gov.  This ruling does not address the applicability of any additional duties, taxes, fees, exactions and/or other  charges, which may apply to the goods discussed herein. This includes, but is not limited to, tariffs and other  duties as provided for in Subchapter III to Chapter 99, HTSUS. Thus, for example, in addition to the  classification stated above, the merchandise covered by this ruling may also need to be reported with either  the Chapter 99 provision under which an additional tariff applies or one of the Chapter 99 provisions  covering exceptions to such tariffs.  For further information to assist with the importation process, please refer to the frequently updated Cargo  Systems Messaging Service (CSMS) messages at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/automated/cargo-systems-messaging-service and the Trade Remedies page at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/programs-administration/trade-remedies.
