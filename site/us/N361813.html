Konu

The tariff classification of a religious book from Israel

Eşyanın Tanımı (Türkçe özet)

26 Mayıs 2026 tarihli yazınızda basılı dini kitaplara ilişkin tarife tasnifi kararı talep etmiştiniz. İncelememiz için açıklama ve bir örnek gönderildi. Karar talebinizde, malların geleneksel İbranice öğelerden, özellikle dua kitapları, dini kitaplar, İnciller ve dua ve çalışma amaçlı dini kitaplardan oluştuğunu belirtirsiniz. Ancak elimize sadece bir örnek geldi, o da sizin "İbranice Mezmurlar Kitabı (Tehillim)" olarak tanımladığınız. Bu karar, aldığımız numuneye göre verilecektir. Söz konusu eser, sizin "İbranice Mezmurlar Kitabı (Tehillim)" olarak tanımladığınız, yumuşak kapaklı bir kitaptır. Kitap 128 sayfa İbranice metin içeriyor, poliüretandan yapılmış suni deri kapağa sahip ve yaklaşık 8,25 cm x 11,5 cm boyutlarında. Kâr amacı gütmeyen, açık kaynaklı bir dijital kütüphane olan sefaria.org web sitesine göre, İbranice İncil'in Yazılar adlı bölümünün ilk kitabı olan "Mezmurlar ("Tehillim"), Kral Davut'a ve diğerlerine atfedilen 150 şiirden oluşan bir antolojidir. Tanrı'ya övgü şarkıları, toplumsal veya kişisel trajediye ilişkin ağıtlar ve öfke, umutsuzluk, umut ve şükran ifadelerini içerir. Mezmurlar, Yahudi ayinlerinde yaygındır ve genellikle bağımsız olarak okunur. Genellikle şarkı söylenir veya söylenir.

Sınıflandırmanın Gerekçesi (Türkçe özet)

“İbranice Mezmurlar Kitabı (Tehillim)” kitabı için geçerli alt başlık, İnciller, vasiyetnameler, dua kitapları ve diğer dini kitapları sağlayan 4901.99.0040, Amerika Birleşik Devletleri Uyumlaştırılmış Tarife Çizelgesi (HTSUS) olacaktır. Genel vergi oranı ücretsiz olacaktır.

  Chaim Greenzeig  Orot Ludaica LLC  6701 Franklin St  Hollywood, FL 33024  RE:  The tariff classification of a religious book from Israel  Dear Mr. Greenzeig:  In your letter dated May 26, 2026, you requested a tariff classification ruling on a printed religious book.  D  escription and a sample were submitted for our review.  In your ruling request, you indicate the goods consist of traditional Hebrew items, specifically prayer books,  religious books, Bibles, and religious books intended for prayer and study.  However, we only received one  sample, which you identified as the “Hebrew Book of Psalms (Tehillim).”  This ruling will be based on the  sample we received.  The item under consideration is a softcover book, which you identified as the “Hebrew Book of Psalms  (Tehillim).”  The book contains 128 pages of Hebrew text, features a faux leather cover made of  polyurethane, and measures approximately 8.25 cm x 11.5 cm.  According to the website sefaria.org, a  non-profit, open-source digital library, “Psalms (“Tehillim”), the first book of the section in the Hebrew  Bible called Writings, is an anthology of 150 poems attributed to King David and to others. It includes songs  of praise to God, laments of communal or personal tragedy, and expressions of anger, despair, hope, and  gratitude. Psalms are prevalent throughout Jewish liturgy and commonly recited as an independent form of  prayer. They are often sung or chanted.”     The applicable subheading for the book, “Hebrew Book of Psalms (Tehillim),” will be 4901.99.0040,  Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS), which provides for, Bibles, testaments, prayer  books and other religious books.  The general rate of duty will be free.  The duties cited above are current as of this ruling’s issuance.  Duty rates are provided for your convenience  and are subject to change. The text of the most recent HTSUS and the accompanying duty rates are provided  at https://hts.usitc.gov/. 

This ruling does not address the applicability of any additional duties, taxes, fees, exactions and/or other  charges, which may apply to the goods discussed herein.  This includes, but is not limited to, tariffs and other  duties as provided for in Subchapter III to Chapter 99, HTSUS.  Thus, for example, in addition to the  classification stated above, the merchandise covered by this ruling may also need to be reported with either  the Chapter 99 provision under which an additional tariff applies or one of the Chapter 99 provisions  covering exceptions to such tariffs.  For further information to assist with the importation process, please refer to the frequently updated Cargo  Systems Messaging Service (CSMS) messages at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/automated/cargo-systems-messaging-service and the Trade Remedies page at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/programs-administration/trade-remedies.  The holding set forth above applies only to the specific factual situation and merchandise description as  identified in the ruling request. This section states that a ruling letter is issued on the assumption that all of the  information furnished in the ruling letter, whether directly, by reference, or by implication, is accurate and  complete in every material respect.
