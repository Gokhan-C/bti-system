The tariff classification of two decorative headbands from China

Eşyanın Tanımı (Türkçe özet)

1 Temmuz 2026 tarihli yazınızda tarife sınıflandırma kararı talep etmiştiniz. Örnek yerine görseller sunulmuştur. Söz konusu ürünler iki dekoratif saç bandıdır. Her ikisi de tamamen siyah dokuma polyester kumaşla kaplanmış yarı sert metal at nalı şeklindeki çerçeveden oluşur. Kafa bantlarından birinde grogren kaplamalı büyük siyah polyester fiyonk bulunurken, diğerinde yıldız şeklinde küçük plastik süslemeli polyester tül ağdan yapılmış siyah bir duvak bulunur. Yazışmanıza göre iki saç bandı birlikte ithal edilecek ve satılacaktır. Talebinizde, kafa bantlarının 9615.19.6000, Amerika Birleşik Devletleri Harmonize Tarife Tablosu (HTSUS) alt pozisyonu altında sınıflandırılmasını öneriyorsunuz. Biz de katılıyoruz. Bu baş bantları, kullanıcının saçını tutmak gibi faydacı bir işlev görür ve 9615 pozisyonunda yer alan saç tokaları olarak kabul edilir. Kumaş bileşenleri bu baş bantlarının her ikisine de görsel çekicilik kazandırdığından, bu makam bunların temel karakteri kazandıracağı görüşündedir, Yorum Genel Kuralı 6 ve 3(b)'de belirtilmiştir. İki dekoratif saç bandı için geçerli sınıflandırma 9615.19.6000, HTSUS olacaktır ve bu sınıflandırma şu şekilde olacaktır: "Taraklar, tokalar ve benzerleri; saç tokaları, saç tokaları, kıvırma sapları, saç kıvırıcıları ve benzerleri (8516 pozisyonundakiler hariç) ve bunların parçaları: Taraklar, tokalar ve benzerleri: Diğer: Diğer." Birinci sütunda genel vergi oranı yüzde 11'dir. Yukarıda belirtilen görevler bu kararın yayımlandığı tarih itibarıyla geçerlidir. Görev oranları size kolaylık sağlamak amacıyla verilmiştir ve değişebilir. En güncel HTSUS metni ve beraberindeki vergi oranları https://hts.usitc.gov/ adresinde sunulmaktadır.

Bu karar herhangi bir ek verginin, verginin uygulanabilirliğine değinmemektedir.

  Hsiao Hsuan Lin  In the Mood Living PTE. LTD.  60 Paya Lebar Road  #06-28, Paya Lebar Square  Singapore 409051  RE:  The tariff classification of two decorative headbands from China  Dear Ms. Lin:  In your letter dated July 1, 2026, you requested a tariff classification ruling.  Images were provided in lieu of a sample.  The articles under consideration are two decorative headbands.  Both consist of a semi-rigid metal  horseshoe-shaped frame covered entirely with a black woven polyester fabric.  One headband features a large  black polyester bow with a grosgrain finish, while the other features a black veil made of polyester tulle  netting with small plastic embellishments in the shape of stars.  Per your correspondence, the two headbands  will be imported and sold together.      In your request, you propose classification of the headbands under subheading 9615.19.6000, Harmonized  Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS).  We agree.  These headbands serve the utilitarian function of  holding the wearer’s hair and would be considered hair-slides of heading 9615.  As the fabric components  imbue both of these headbands with their visual appeal, this office is of the opinion they would impart the  essential character, General Rule of Interpretation 6 and 3(b) noted.  The applicable classification for the two decorative headbands will be 9615.19.6000, HTSUS, which  provides for “Combs, hair-slides and the like; hairpins, curling pins, curling grips, hair curlers and the like,  other than those of heading 8516, and parts thereof: Combs, hair-slides and the like: Other: Other.”  The  column one, general rate of duty is 11 percent ad valorem.  The duties cited above are current as of this ruling’s issuance.  Duty rates are provided for your convenience  and are subject to change.  The text of the most recent HTSUS and the accompanying duty rates are provided  at https://hts.usitc.gov/. 

This ruling does not address the applicability of any additional duties, taxes, fees, exactions and/or other  charges, which may apply to the goods discussed herein.  This includes, but is not limited to, tariffs and other  duties as provided for in Subchapter III to Chapter 99, HTSUS.  Thus, for example, in addition to the  classification stated above, the merchandise covered by this ruling may also need to be reported with either  the Chapter 99 provision under which an additional tariff applies or one of the Chapter 99 provisions  covering exceptions to such tariffs.  For further information to assist with the importation process, please refer to the frequently updated Cargo  Systems Messaging Service (CSMS) messages at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/automated/cargo-systems-messaging-service and Frequently Asked Questions on  the Trade Remedy/IEEPA page at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/programs-administration/trade-remedies/IEEPA-FAQ.  The holding set forth above applies only to the specific factual situation and merchandise description as  identified in the ruling request.  This position is clearly set forth in Title 19, Code of Federal Regulations  (CFR), Section 177.9(b)(1).  This section states that a ruling letter is issued on the assumption that all of the  information furnished in the ruling letter, whether directly, by reference, or by implication, is accurate and  complete in every material respect.  In the event that the facts are modified in any way, or if the goods do not  conform to these facts at time of importation, you should bring this to the attention of U.S. Customs and  Border Protection (CBP) and submit a request for a new ruling in accordance with 19 CFR 177.2.   Additionally, we note that the material facts described in the foregoing ruling may be subject to periodic  verification by CBP.  This ruling is being issued under the provisions of Part 177 of the Customs and Border Protection  Regulations (19 C.F.R. 177).  A copy of the ruling or the control number indicated above should be provided with the entry documents  filed at the time this merchandise is imported.
