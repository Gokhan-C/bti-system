Konu

The tariff classification of a tray filler from Denmark

Eşyanın Tanımı (Türkçe özet)

Müşteriniz Blackmore Co. adına 10 Haziran 2026 tarihli mektubunuzda, tesis alt tabaka tepsi doldurucularına ilişkin tarife sınıflandırma kararı talep etmiştiniz. İncelememiz için tanımlayıcı literatür sağlandı. Söz konusu ürün, sera içi veya dışı ortamlar için tasarlanmış endüstriyel ölçekte bir tepsi doldurma hattı olan Ellepot 2.0 Pot Filler (aynı zamanda Paper Pot 2.0 olarak da anılır) olarak tanımlanmaktadır. Sistem bir ana çerçeve (kontrol ünitesi barındıran), entegre 3.000 mm'lik tepsi konveyör hattı, bir kağıt rulosu çerçevesi, bir kağıt çekme/yakalama tertibatı, bir pnömatik bıçak/testere bıçağı kesme ünitesi ve teleskopik boru sistemi ve mekanik karıştırma kolu içeren bir toprak-alt tabaka ölçüm ünitesinden oluşur. Çalışma sırasında 600x400 mm'ye kadar boş hücre tepsileri konveyör bandına yüklenir. Tesis substratı (toprak karışımı), eşlik eden bir dökme huniden/toprak karıştırıcısından makinenin giriş terminaline beslenir (saatte 4 metreküp'e kadar işleme). Eş zamanlı olarak kağıt besleme ünitesi, entegre bir huni araç seti kullanarak tek tek kağıt astarlarını tepsi hücrelerine doğru ilerletir, keser ve katlar. Teleskopik tüpler, kağıt kaplı hücrelere hassas miktarda toprak substratını mekanik olarak ölçer. Doldurulan “kağıt kaplar” daha sonra tepsi konveyörü aracılığıyla sistematik olarak dışarı beslenir. Ellepot 2.0 Kap Doldurucu, birkaç farklı başlıkta sınıflandırılabilecek çok sayıda farklı bileşen (taşıyıcılar, kesme makineleri, kağıt rulosu besleme makineleri vb.) içerse de, makinenin genel olarak temel işlevi, yüksek hacimli kap doldurma işlevini gerçekleştirmektir.   Sidestra Elvi  U.S. Logistics Group, Inc.  841 Fairmount Ave  Elizabeth, NJ 07207  RE:  The tariff classification of a tray filler from Denmark  Dear Ms. Elvi:  In your letter dated June 10, 2026, on behalf of your client, Blackmore Co., you requested a tariff  classification ruling on plant substrate tray fillers. Descriptive literature was provided for our review.  The item under consideration is described as the Ellepot 2.0 Pot Filler (also referred to as the Paper Pot 2.0),  which is an industrial-scale tray-filling line designed for inside or outside greenhouse environments. The  system consists of a main frame (housing a control unit), an integrated 3,000 mm tray conveyor line, a paper  roll frame, a paper pulling/grabbing assembly, a pneumatic knife/sawblade cutting unit, and a soil-substrate  measuring unit incorporating a telescopic tube system and mechanical stirring arm. In operation, empty cell  trays up to 600x400 mm are loaded onto the conveyor belt. Plant substrate (soil mix) is fed from a companion  bulk hopper/soil mixer into an entry terminal of the machine (processing up to 4 cubic meters per hour).  Simultaneously, the paper feeding unit advances, cuts, and folds individual paper liners down into the tray  cells utilizing an integrated funnel toolset. The telescopic tubes mechanically meter a precise volume of soil  substrate into the paper-lined cells. The filled “paper pots” are then systematically fed out via the tray  conveyor.  While the Ellepot 2.0 Pot Filler contains numerous different components that would be classified in several  different headings (such as conveyers, cutting machines, paper roll feeding machines, etc.), the principal  function of the overall machine is to perform a high-volume container filling function.  Therefore, in accordance with Section XVI Note 4, the applicable subheading for the Ellepot 2.0 Pot Filler  will be 8422.30.9191, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS), which provides for  “Dishwashing machines; machinery for cleaning or drying bottles or other containers; machinery for filling,  closing, sealing or labeling bottles, cans, boxes, bags or other containers; machinery for capsuling bottles,  jars, tubes and similar containers; other packing or wrapping machinery (including heat-shrink wrapping  machinery); machinery for aerating beverages; parts thereof: Machinery for filling, closing, sealing or 

labeling bottles, cans, boxes, bags or other containers; machinery for capsuling bottles, jars, tubes and similar  containers; machinery for aerating beverages: Other: Other: Machinery for filling, closing, sealing, capsuling  or labeling boxes, bags or similar containers: Other.” The general rate of duty will be free.  The duties cited above are current as of this ruling’s issuance.  Duty rates are provided for your convenience  and are subject to change. The text of the most recent HTSUS and the accompanying duty rates are provided  at https://hts.usitc.gov/.  This ruling does not address the applicability of any additional duties, taxes, fees, exactions and/or other  charges, which may apply to the goods discussed herein.  This includes, but is not limited to, tariffs and other  duties as provided for in Subchapter III to Chapter 99, HTSUS.  Thus, for example, in addition to the  classification stated above, the merchandise covered by this ruling may also need to be reported with either  the Chapter 99 provision under which an additional tariff applies or one of the Chapter 99 provisions  covering exceptions to such tariffs.  For further information to assist with the importation process, please refer to the frequently updated Cargo  Systems Messaging Service (CSMS) messages at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/automated/cargo-systems-messaging-service and the Trade Remedies page at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/programs-administration/trade-remedies.  The holding set forth above applies only to the specific factual situation and merchandise description as  identified in the ruling request.  This position is clearly set forth in Title 19, Code of Federal Regulations  (CFR), Section 177.9(b)(1).  This section states that a ruling letter is issued on the assumption that all of the  information furnished in the ruling letter, whether directly, by reference, or by implication, is accurate and  complete in every material respect.  In the event that the facts are modified in any way, or if the goods do not  conform to these facts at time of importation, you should bring this to the attention of U.S. Customs and  Border Protection (CBP) and submit a request for a new ruling in accordance with 19 CFR 177.2.   Additionally, we note that the material facts described in the foregoing ruling may be subject to periodic  verification by CBP.  This ruling is being issued under the provisions of Part 177 of the Customs and Border Protection  Regulations (19 C.F.R. 177).
