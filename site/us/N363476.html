Konu

The tariff classification of a RoboMIDI and RoboMAX from Italy

Eşyanın Tanımı (Türkçe özet)

21 Temmuz 2026 tarihli yazınızda tarife sınıflandırma kararı talep etmiştiniz. Söz konusu ürün RoboMIDI ve RoboMAX olarak tanımlanıyor; siz bunları öncelikli olarak ormancılık ve yabani bitki örtüsü kontrolü için tasarlanmış ve pazarlanan iki paletli, uzaktan kumandalı alet taşıyıcısı olarak tanımlıyorsunuz. RoboMIDI ve RoboMAX, yaklaşık 492 feet çalışma aralığına sahip, çok işlevli bir uzaktan kumandayla çalıştırılan, hareketin, direksiyonun ve ataşman konumlandırmanın hassas kontrolüne olanak tanıyan dizel motorlu taşıyıcılardır. Her iki model de çeşitli arazilere uyum sağlamak için çoklu palet konfigürasyonlarıyla sunulmaktadır. Makineler, kamuya açık yollarda mal veya yolcu taşımak için tasarlanmamıştır ve çekme veya itme amaçlı tasarlanmamıştır. İşlevleri, öne monteli çalışma araçlarıyla gerçekleştirilen görevlerle sınırlıdır. Her model bir dizi ataşmanı kabul edebilse de, bunların tasarımı, yapımı ve pazarlanması, ormancılık ve yabani bitki örtüsü yönetiminde temel bir amacı göstermektedir. RoboMIDI genellikle sap parçalama ve ormancılık kafaları, kütük öğütücüler, vinçler, hendek kazıcılar ve biyo-parçalayıcılar (yontucular) taşır. RoboMAX tipik olarak ormancılık kafaları (sallanan çekiçler veya sabit dişler), çalılar için bir çim/fırça kafası, budama diskleri, biyolojik öğütücüler, kütük öğütücüler ve taş kırıcılarla ve isteğe bağlı bir püskürtücüyle yapılandırılır. ABD'li müşterilerin ağırlıklı olarak bu makineleri ormancılık kafalarıyla eşleştirdiğini ve makinelerin bu aletlerle birlikte ithal edildiğini ifade ediyorsunuz. Ormancılık ataşmanları olmadan makineler amaçlanan işlevini yerine getiremez. Ataşmanlar lojistik amacıyla ayrı olarak gönderilebilse de, makinelerle birlikte ithal edilir ve ormancılıkta amaçlanan kullanımın ayrılmaz bir parçasıdır.

Sınıflandırmanın Gerekçesi (Türkçe özet)

RoboMIDI ve RoboMAX için geçerli alt başlık, 8436.80.0020, Amerika Birleşik Devletleri Uyumlaştırılmış Tarife Tablosu (HTSUS) olacaktır; bu, mekanik veya termal sistemlerle donatılmış çimlendirme tesisi de dahil olmak üzere diğer tarım, bahçecilik, ormancılık, kümes hayvanı yetiştirme veya arıcılık makinelerini sağlar.

  Alessandro Cazzin  Energreen America Inc.  227 Trade Court  Aiken, SC 29805  RE:  The tariff classification of a RoboMIDI and RoboMAX from Italy  Dear Mr. Cazzin:  In your letter dated July 21, 2026, you requested a tariff classification ruling.  The merchandise under consideration is identified as the RoboMIDI and RoboMAX, which you describe as  two tracked, remote-controlled tool carriers that are designed and marketed primarily for forestry and wild  vegetation control.  The RoboMIDI and RoboMAX are diesel-powered carriers operated by a multifunction  remote with a working range of approximately 492 feet, allowing precise control of movement, steering, and  attachment positioning.  Both models are offered with multiple track configurations to adapt to varied terrain.  The machines are not intended for the transportation of goods or passengers over public roads and are not  designed for hauling or pushing. Their functionality is limited to tasks performed via front-mounted work  tools.  Although each model can accept a range of attachments, their design, construction, and marketing  demonstrate a principal purpose in forestry and wild vegetation management.  The RoboMIDI commonly  carries mulcher and forestry heads, stump grinders, winches, trenchers, and bio-shredders (chippers). The  RoboMAX is typically configured with forestry heads (swinging hammers or fixed teeth), a grass/brush head  for shrubs, pruner discs, bio-shredders, stump grinders, and stone crushers, with an optional sprayer.  You state that U.S. customers overwhelmingly pair these machines with forestry heads, and the machines are  imported together with those tools.  Without forestry attachments, the machines cannot perform their  intended function.  While the attachments may be shipped detached for logistics, they are imported together  with the machines and are integral to their intended forestry use.  The applicable subheading for the RoboMIDI and RoboMAX will be 8436.80.0020, Harmonized Tariff  Schedule of the United States (HTSUS), which provides for Other agricultural, horticultural, forestry,  poultry-keeping or bee-keeping machinery, including germination plant fitted with mechanical or thermal 

equipment; poultry incubators and brooders; parts thereof: Other machinery: Forestry machinery. The general  rate of duty will be free.  The duties cited above are current as of this ruling’s issuance.  Duty rates are provided for your convenience  and are subject to change. The text of the most recent HTSUS and the accompanying duty rates are provided  at https://hts.usitc.gov/.  This ruling does not address the applicability of any additional duties, taxes, fees, exactions and/or other  charges, which may apply to the goods discussed herein.  This includes, but is not limited to, tariffs and other  duties as provided for in Subchapter III to Chapter 99, HTSUS.  Thus, for example, in addition to the  classification stated above, the merchandise covered by this ruling may also need to be reported with either  the Chapter 99 provision under which an additional tariff applies or one of the Chapter 99 provisions  covering exceptions to such tariffs.  For further information to assist with the importation process, please refer to the frequently updated Cargo  Systems Messaging Service (CSMS) messages at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/automated/cargo-systems-messaging-service and the Trade Remedies page at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/programs-administration/trade-remedies.  The holding set forth above applies only to the specific factual situation and merchandise description as  identified in the ruling request.  This position is clearly set forth in Title 19, Code of Federal Regulations  (CFR), Section 177.9(b)(1).  This section states that a ruling letter is issued on the assumption that all of the  information furnished in the ruling letter, whether directly, by reference, or by implication, is accurate and  complete in every material respect.  In the event that the facts are modified in any way, or if the goods do not  conform to these facts at time of importation, you should bring this to the attention of U.S. Customs and  Border Protection (CBP) and submit a request for a new ruling in accordance with 19 CFR 177.2.  Additionally, we note that the material facts described in the foregoing ruling may be subject to periodic  verification by CBP.  This ruling is being issued under the provisions of Part 177 of the Customs and Border Protection  Regulations (19 C.F.R. 177).  A copy of the ruling or the control number indicated above should be provided with the entry documents  filed at the time this merchandise is imported.
