Konu

The tariff classification of a roof rack system from Germany

Eşyanın Tanımı (Türkçe özet)

3 Temmuz 2026 tarihli yazınızda tarife sınıflandırma kararı talebinde bulunmuşsunuz. Söz konusu ürün, belirli bir araç için tasarlanmış ve yapılandırılmış bir tavan rafı sistemidir. “SpaceRack” isimli ürün, EN AW-6063 T66 alaşımından ekstrüde edilmiş alüminyum profillerden, AlMg3 (EN AW-5754) alaşımından lazer CNC kesimli alüminyum plakalardan ve paslanmaz çelik bileşenlerden ve bağlantı elemanlarından (ağırlıkça yaklaşık %70 alüminyum ve %30 paslanmaz çelik) oluşmaktadır. Bileşenler araca özgü teknik özelliklere göre lazerle kesilmiştir. Sistem, kurulum için gerekli tüm parçaları içeren komple bir kit halinde demonte olarak ithal edilmektedir. Monte edildikten sonra cihaz, belirli bir motorlu taşıtın tavanına kalıcı olarak monte edilir ve o taşıt üzerindeki yükleri taşımaya yarar. T

Sınıflandırmanın Gerekçesi (Türkçe özet)

"SpaceRack" için geçerli alt pozisyon, 8701 ila 8705 pozisyonlarındaki motorlu taşıtların parça ve aksesuarlarını öngören Amerika Birleşik Devletleri Uyumlaştırılmış Tarife Tablosu (HTSUS) 8708.29.5190 olacaktır: Karoserlerin diğer parçaları ve aksesuarları (kabinler dahil): Diğer: Diğer: Diğer." Genel vergi oranı değer üzerinden yüzde 2,5 olacaktır.

  Florian Zinkl  Rolling Space GmbH & Co. KG  Hauptstraße 6  Winklarn 92559  Germany  RE:  The tariff classification of a roof rack system from Germany  Dear Mr. Zinkl:  In your letter dated July 3, 2026, you requested a tariff classification ruling.  The article under consideration is a roof rack system engineered and configured for a specific vehicle.  The product named “SpaceRack” is composed of extruded aluminum profiles of alloy EN AW-6063 T66,  laser CNC-cut aluminum plates of alloy AlMg3 (EN AW-5754), and stainless steel components and fasteners  (approximately 70% aluminum and 30% stainless steel by weight). The components are laser-cut to vehicle  unique specifications. The system is imported unassembled as one complete kit, containing all necessary  parts required for installation. Once assembled, the device is permanently mounted on the roof of the specific  motor vehicle and serves to carry loads on that vehicle.  The applicable subheading for the “SpaceRack” will be 8708.29.5190, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the  United States (HTSUS), which provides for Parts and accessories of the motor vehicles of headings 8701 to  8705: Other parts and accessories of bodies (including cabs): Other: Other: Other.” The general rate of duty  will be 2.5 percent ad valorem.  The duties cited above are current as of this ruling’s issuance.  Duty rates are provided for your convenience  and are subject to change. The text of the most recent HTSUS and the accompanying duty rates are provided  at https://hts.usitc.gov/.  This ruling does not address the applicability of any additional duties, taxes, fees, exactions and/or other  charges, which may apply to the goods discussed herein.  This includes, but is not limited to, tariffs and other  duties as provided for in Subchapter III to Chapter 99, HTSUS.  Thus, for example, in addition to the  classification stated above, the merchandise covered by this ruling may also need to be reported with either 

the Chapter 99 provision under which an additional tariff applies or one of the Chapter 99 provisions  covering exceptions to such tariffs.  For further information to assist with the importation process, please refer to the frequently updated Cargo  Systems Messaging Service (CSMS) messages at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/automated/cargo-systems-messaging-service and the Trade Remedies page at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/programs-administration/trade-remedies.  The holding set forth above applies only to the specific factual situation and merchandise description as  identified in the ruling request.  This position is clearly set forth in Title 19, Code of Federal Regulations  (CFR), Section 177.9(b)(1).  This section states that a ruling letter is issued on the assumption that all of the  information furnished in the ruling letter, whether directly, by reference, or by implication, is accurate and  complete in every material respect.  In the event that the facts are modified in any way, or if the goods do not  conform to these facts at time of importation, you should bring this to the attention of U.S. Customs and  Border Protection (CBP) and submit a request for a new ruling in accordance with 19 CFR 177.2.  Additionally, we note that the material facts described in the foregoing ruling may be subject to periodic  verification by CBP.  This ruling is being issued under the provisions of Part 177 of the Customs and Border Protection  Regulations (19 C.F.R. 177).  A copy of the ruling or the control number indicated above should be provided with the entry documents  filed at the time this merchandise is imported.
