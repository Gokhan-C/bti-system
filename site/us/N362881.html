Konu

The tariff classification of deadend connectors from Mexico

Eşyanın Tanımı (Türkçe özet)

6 Temmuz 2026 tarihli mektubunuzda tarife sınıflandırma kararı talep etmişsiniz ve müşteriniz Hubbell Power Systems, Inc. adına tarife sınıflandırma kararı talep etmişsiniz. Bu taleple birlikte değerlendirilen dört öğe bulunmaktadır; bunların tümü yüksek gerilim havai enerji iletim veya dağıtım sistemlerinde kullanıma yönelik kör uçlu konnektörler olarak tanımlanır ve ayrı ayrı (Madde 1) Etriye Kelepçeli Alüminyum Cıvatalı Çıkmaz (Model No. ADES70N) (“Etriye Modeli”); (Madde 2) Düz Çizgili, Yaylı, Yandan Açılır Alüminyum Cıvatalı Çıkmaz (Model No. ADEZ116N) (“Yaylı Model”); (Madde 3) Düz Çizgi, Gergi Kelepçesi Alüminyum Cıvatalı Çıkmaz Uç (Model No. ADS88N) (“Alüminyum Model”); ve (Madde 4) Alüminyum Çeyrek Daire Gergi Kelepçesi Cıvatalı Çıkmaz Uç (Model No. SD185N) (“Çeyrek Daire Modeli”). Madde 1, Üzengi Modeli, alüminyumdan, çelik takviyeli alüminyumdan veya alüminyum alaşımından yapılmış dağıtım iletkenleriyle birlikte kullanılır. Elektrik bağlantısı yapmak amacıyla iletkenleri yerinde sabitlemek için kullanılan iki U cıvata içerir. Gövde ve kaleci alüminyum alaşımdan, üzengi bakırdan yapılmıştır. Elektrik, iletkenden kör uçlu konektör aracılığıyla üzengi demirine akar; burada bir sıcak hat kelepçesi, aşağı yöndeki bir elektrikli aparata (örneğin, anahtar, kapasitör bankası, koruma cihazı) takılabilir. Madde 2, Yaylı Model, alüminyumdan, çelik takviyeli alüminyumdan veya alüminyum alaşımından yapılmış dağıtım ve ışık iletim iletkenleriyle birlikte kullanılır. Yaylı bir tutucuya sahip bir yan açıklık ve elektrik bağlantısı yapmak için iletkenleri yerine sabitlemek için kullanılan iki U cıvata içerir. Gövde ve koruyucu, elektriğin güvenli ve verimli akışını sağlayan alüminyum alaşımdan yapılmıştır. Elektrik borudan akıyor

Sınıflandırmanın Gerekçesi (Türkçe özet)

Dört kör uçlu konnektör modeli için geçerli alt pozisyon, “1.000 V'u aşan bir voltaj için… elektrik devrelerini anahtarlamak veya korumak veya elektrik devrelerine veya devrelerde bağlantı yapmak için kullanılan elektrikli cihazlar: Diğer: Diğer: Diğer konnektörleri” öngören 8535.90.8040, HTSUS olacaktır. Genel vergi oranı ad valorem olarak yüzde 2,7 olacaktır.

  Michael Murphy  Sidley Austin LLP  1501 K St. NW  Washington, D.C. 20005  RE:  The tariff classification of deadend connectors from Mexico  Dear Mr. Murphy:  In your letter dated July 6, 2026, you requested a tariff classification ruling you requested a tariff  classification ruling on behalf of your client, Hubbell Power Systems, Inc.  There are four items under consideration with this request, which are all identified as deadend connectors for  use in high voltage overhead power transmission or distribution systems and are individually identified as  (Item 1) Aluminum Bolted Deadend with Stirrup Clamp (Model No. ADES70N) (“the Stirrup Model”); (Item  2) Straight Line, Spring-Loaded, Side-Opening Aluminum Bolted Deadend (Model No. ADEZ116N) (“the  Spring-Loaded Model”); (Item 3) Straight Line, Strain Clamp Aluminum Bolted Deadend (Model No.  ADS88N) (“the Aluminum Model”); and (Item 4) Aluminum Quadrant Strain Clamp Bolted Deadend  (Model No. SD185N) (“the Quadrant Model”).  Item 1, the Stirrup Model, is used with distribution conductors made of aluminum, steel reinforced  aluminum, or aluminum alloy. It includes two U-bolts which are used to secure conductors in place to make  an electrical connection. The body and keeper are made of aluminum alloy, and the stirrup is made of copper.  Electricity flows from the conductor through the deadend connector to the stirrup, where a hot line clamp can  be attached to a downstream electrical apparatus (e.g., switch, capacitor bank, protection device).  Item 2, the Spring-Loaded Model, is used with distribution and light transmission conductors made of  aluminum, steel reinforced aluminum, or aluminum alloy. It includes a side opening with a spring-loaded  keeper and two U-bolts used to secure conductors in place to make an electrical connection. The body and  keeper are made of aluminum alloy, allowing for the safe and efficient flow of electricity. Electricity flows  from the conductor through the deadend connector to allow for a connection to be made to other parts of the  grid (e.g., insulators, fuse disconnect switches, lightning arrestors, or distribution transformers) using a clamp  or similar connector. 

Item 3, the Aluminum Model, is used with distribution and light transmission conductors made of aluminum.  It includes two U-bolts which are used to secure conductors in place to make an electrical connection. This  model is manufactured using nonferrous conductive aluminum alloy. Electricity flows from the conductor  through the deadend connector to allow for a connection to be made to other parts of the grid (e.g., insulators,  fuse disconnect switches, lightning arrestors, or distribution transformers) using a clamp or similar connector.  Item 4, the Quadrant Model, is used with transmission conductors made of aluminum. It includes five U-bolts  to secure the conductors in place to complete the electrical connection. The body and keeper are made of  aluminum alloy. Electricity flows from the conductor through the deadend connector to allow for a  connection to be made to other parts of the grid (e.g., insulators, fuse disconnect switches, lightning arrestors,  or distribution transformers) using a clamp or similar connector.  In your request, you suggest that the correct classification for the subject deadends is under subheading  8535.90.8040, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS). We agree.  The applicable subheading for all four models of deadend connectors will be 8535.90.8040, HTSUS, which  provides for “Electrical apparatus for switching or protecting electrical circuits, or for making connections to  or in electrical circuits…for a voltage exceeding 1,000 V: Other: Other: Other connectors.” The general rate  of duty will be 2.7 percent ad valorem.  The duties cited above are current as of this ruling’s issuance.  Duty rates are provided for your convenience  and are subject to change. The text of the most recent HTSUS and the accompanying duty rates are provided  at https://hts.usitc.gov/.  This ruling does not address the applicability of any additional duties, taxes, fees, exactions and/or other  charges, which may apply to the goods discussed herein.  This includes, but is not limited to, tariffs and other  duties as provided for in Subchapter III to Chapter 99, HTSUS.  Thus, for example, in addition to the  classification stated above, the merchandise covered by this ruling may also need to be reported with either  the Chapter 99 provision under which an additional tariff applies or one of the Chapter 99 provisions  covering exceptions to such tariffs.  For further information to assist with the importation process, please refer to the frequently updated Cargo  Systems Messaging Service (CSMS) messages at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/automated/cargo-systems-messaging-service and the Trade Remedies page at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/programs-administration/trade-remedies.  The holding set forth above applies only to the specific factual situation and merchandise description as  identified in the ruling request.  This position is clearly set forth in Title 19, Code of Federal Regulations  (CFR), Section 177.9(b)(1).  This section states that a ruling letter is issued on the assumption that all of the  information furnished in the ruling letter, whether directly, by reference, or by implication, is accurate and  complete in every material respect.  In the event that the facts are modified in any way, or if the goods do not  conform to these facts at time of importation, you should bring this to the attention of U.S. Customs and  Border Protection (CBP) and submit a request for a new ruling in accordance with 19 CFR 177.2.   Additionally, we note that the material facts described in the foregoing ruling may be subject to periodic  verification by CBP.  This ruling is being issued under the provisions of Part 177 of the Customs and Border Protection  Regulations (19 C.F.R. 177). 

A copy of the ruling or the control number indicated above should be provided with the entry documents  filed at the time this merchandise is imported. If you have any questions regarding the ruling, please contact  National Import Specialist Gary Chaffee at gary.chaffee@cbp.dhs.gov.
