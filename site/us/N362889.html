Konu

The tariff classification of seats from Cambodia

Eşyanın Tanımı (Türkçe özet)

6 Temmuz 2026 tarihli mektubunuzda True Innovations and Design LLC adına tarife sınıflandırma kararı talep etmişsiniz. Örneklerin yerine inceleme için teknik ve açıklayıcı literatür, ürün açıklamaları ve üretim özeti sağlandı. Öğe 1, “Renwick Kanepe (model 3668E-9173-41Z),” ahşap çerçeveli bir uzanma kanepesidir. Kanepe köpük, polyester elyaf ve deri malzemelerle döşenmiştir; kanepe bir veya daha fazla kişi için oturma alanı sağlayacaktır. Koltuğun yapısal çerçevesi, sırt dayanakları, kol dayama yerleri ve ayak dayama yerleri düz, içbükey ve dışbükey ahşap panellerden yapılmıştır. Kanepe, ahşap çerçeveye takılan, monte edilen ve entegre edilen bir çift metal yaslanma mekanizmasına sahiptir. Kanepenin sol ve sağ koltuk başlıklarını hareket ettiren bir çift elektrik motoru vardır. Sol ve sağ kanepe bacak dayanakları geri çekilebilir, kanepe geriye doğru yattığından bacak dayanakları koltuk temelinden uzağa doğru uzanır. Koltuk eğildikçe sol ve sağ bacak desteği koltuk temeline geri döner. Kanepe boyutları 87" uzunluk, 41" derinlik ve 42" yüksekliktir. Öğe 2, "Renwick Recliner (model 3668E-9173—81HZ)," ahşap çerçeveli bir uzanma sandalyesidir. Sandalye köpük, polyester elyaf ve deri malzemelerle döşenmiştir; sandalye tek bir kişi için oturma alanı sağlayacaktır. Koltuğun yapısal çerçevesi, arkalığı, kol dayama yerleri ve ayak dayaması düz, içbükey ve dışbükey ahşap paneller. Sandalye, ahşap çerçeveye takılan, monte edilen ve entegre edilen bir metal yatırma mekanizmasına sahiptir. Sandalye, koltuk başlığını hareket ettiren bir elektrik motoruna sahiptir. Koltuk yaslandıkça bacak desteği koltuk tabanından uzağa doğru uzanır.

Sınıflandırmanın Gerekçesi (Türkçe özet)

1. kalem için geçerli alt pozisyon, 9401.61.6011, HTSUS olacaktır ve bu pozisyon, “Yatağa dönüştürülebilir olsun olmasın (9402 pozisyonundakiler hariç) Koltuklar ve bunların parçalarını: Ahşap çerçeveli diğer koltuklar: Döşemeli: Diğer: Diğer ev eşyaları”nı öngörmektedir. Genel vergi oranı ücretsiz olacaktır. 2. kalem için geçerli alt pozisyon 9401.61.4011, HTSUS olacaktır ve bu pozisyon “Yatağa dönüştürülebilir olsun olmasın Koltuklar (9402 pozisyonundakiler hariç) ve bunların parçaları: Ahşap çerçeveli diğer koltuklar: Döşemeli: Sandalyeler: Diğer: Diğer ev eşyaları.” Genel vergi oranı ücretsiz olacaktır.

  Akshay Sinha  Neville Peterson LLP  1310 L Street NW, Suite 300  Washington, DC 20005  RE:  The tariff classification of seats from Cambodia  Dear Akshay Sinha:  In your letter dated July 6, 2026, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of True Innovations and  Design LLC.  In lieu of samples, technical and illustrative literature, product descriptions, and a  manufacturing synopsis were provided for review.  Item 1, the “Renwick Sofa (model 3668E-9173-41Z),” is a wooden frame reclining sofa.  The sofa is  upholstered with foam, polyester fiber, and leather materials; the sofa will provide seating for one or more  individuals.  The seat’s structural frame, backrests, armrests, and the footrests are constructed of straight,  concave, and convex wood panels.  The sofa has a pair of metal recline mechanisms that are inserted,  mounted, and integrated onto the wooden frame.  The sofa has a pair of electric motors that articulate the left  and the right headrests.  The left and right sofa leg rests are retractable, as the sofa reclines the leg rests  extends away from the seat foundation.  As the seat inclines, the left and right leg rest returns to the seat  foundation.  The sofa dimensions are 87" in length, 41" in depth, and 42" in height.  Item 2, the “Renwick Recliner (model 3668E-9173—81HZ),” is a wooden frame reclining chair.  The chair is  upholstered with foam, polyester fiber, and leather materials; the chair will provide seating for a single  individual.  The seat’s structural frame, backrest, armrests, and the footrest are constructed of straight,  concave, and convex wood panels.  The chair has a metal recline mechanism that is inserted, mounted, and  integrated onto the wooden frame.  The chair has an electric motor that articulates the headrest.  The leg rest  is retractable, as the seat reclines the leg rest extends away from the seat foundation.  As the seat inclines, the  leg rest returns to the seat foundation.  The chair’s dimensions are 37.5" in length, 41" in depth, and 42" in  height.  The ruling request seeks classification of item 1 and item 2 in subheading 9401.71.0011 of the Harmonized  Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS).  We disagree. 

The Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System Explanatory Notes (ENs) constitutes the  official interpretation of the Harmonized System at the international level.  EN III to General Rule of Interpretation (GRI) 1 states, “[T]he second part of this Rule provides that  classification shall be determined:  (a) according to the terms of the headings and any relative Section or Chapter Notes, and  (b) where appropriate, provided the headings or Notes do not otherwise require, according to the  provisions of Rule 2, 3, 4, and 5.”  Further, the ENs to Chapter 94, Heading 9401 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule (HTS), for subheading  9401.61 and 9401.71 “Upholstered Seats,” states:  “Upholstered seats are those having a soft layer of, for  example, wadding, tow, animal hair, cellular plastics or rubber, shaped (whether or not fixed) to the seat and  covered with a material such as woven fabric, leather or sheeting of plastics.  Also classified as upholstered  seats are seats the upholstering materials of which are not covered or have only a white fabric cover which is  itself intended to be covered (known as upholstered seats “in muslin”), seats which are presented with  detachable seat or back cushions and which could not be used without such cushions, and seats with helical  springs (for upholstery).  On the other hand, the presence of horizontally-acting tension springs, designed to  attach to the frame a steel wire lattice, taut woven fabric, etc., is not sufficient to cause the seats to be  classified as upholstered.  Similarly, seats covered directly with materials such as woven fabric, leather,  sheeting of plastics, without the interposition of upholstering materials or springs, and seats to which a single  woven fabric backed with a thin layer of cellular plastics has been applied, are not regarded as upholstered  seats.”  Chapter 94, subheading 9401.61, HTS, provides for “Seats (other than those of heading 9402), whether or not  convertible into beds, and parts thereof:  Other seats, with wooden frames:  Upholstered.”   The “Renwick Sofa” and the “Renwick Recliner” are upholstered seats affixed to wooden frames; the terms  of the heading are satisfied.  The applicable subheading for item 1 will be 9401.61.6011, HTSUS, which provides for “Seats (other than  those of heading 9402), whether or not convertible into beds, and parts thereof:  Other seats, with wooden  frames:  Upholstered:  Other:  Other household.” The general rate of duty will be free.  The applicable subheading for item 2 will be 9401.61.4011, HTSUS, which provides for “Seats (other than  those of heading 9402), whether or not convertible into beds, and parts thereof:  Other seats, with wooden  frames:  Upholstered:  Chairs:  Other:  Other household.”  The general rate of duty will be free.  The duties cited above are current as of this ruling’s issuance.  Duty rates are provided for your convenience  and are subject to change. The text of the most recent HTSUS and the accompanying duty rates are provided  at https://hts.usitc.gov/.  This ruling does not address the applicability of any additional duties, taxes, fees, exactions and/or other  charges, which may apply to the goods discussed herein.  This includes, but is not limited to, tariffs and other  duties as provided for in Subchapter III to Chapter 99, HTSUS.  Thus, for example, in addition to the  classification stated above, the merchandise covered by this ruling may also need to be reported with either  the Chapter 99 provision under which an additional tariff applies or one of the Chapter 99 provisions  covering exceptions to such tariffs.  For further information to assist with the importation process, please refer to the frequently updated Cargo  Systems Messaging Service (CSMS) messages at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/automated/cargo-systems-messaging-service and the Trade Remedies page at   https://www.cbp.gov/trade/programs-administration/trade-remedies.
